## Disposition vocabulary

Every research-branch artifact receives exactly one primary disposition before consolidation:

### PROMOTE

Artifact or bounded content is mature enough to become active baseline/canon after verification.

### SYNTHESIZE

Useful evidence exists across one or more exploratory artifacts, but the final baseline should contain a smaller authoritative synthesis rather than the exploratory files themselves.

### RETAIN-AS-RESEARCH

The artifact remains valuable active research/falsification evidence but is not an active architectural authority.

### RELOCATE

The content survives but belongs at another repository layer/path.

### ARCHIVE

The artifact is superseded/rejected/historical evidence worth retaining outside the active library.

### RETIRE

The artifact is obsolete, redundant, generated without durable value, or otherwise unnecessary after its surviving knowledge is preserved elsewhere.

## Governing consolidation laws

1. Do not merge the research branch wholesale.
2. Canon is synthesized from evidence; research history remains distinguishable from active authority.
3. Geometry does not create authority, capability, identity, module count, recognition, governance, Treasury rights, or economic preference by symmetry or numerical fit.
4. Registry/source-owned semantics precede projection.
5. Mathematical definitions, symbolic interpretation, visual rendering, and runtime implementation remain separately typed.
6. A visual representation cannot silently become mathematics.
7. Mathematics cannot silently become doctrine.
8. Symbolic correspondence cannot silently become technical authority.
9. Tempus/astronomical context cannot become permission, destiny, readiness, human-worth scoring, or coercive urgency.
10. Every required geometric experience must have a geometry-free accessible equivalent.
11. Exact historical reasoning may be archived even when the resulting design is rejected.
12. Active docs should be optimized for current understanding and implementation, not for preserving every exploratory step inline.
