# Participant journey and scoped contribution proposal

## Decision provenance and limits

This proposal reconciles selected repository sources at
`a07cebf7e0941a52be8961c349522488cf87b9a3` with the Human Architect's October 7,
2026 instructions in the current chat. The Human confirmed open private
experimentation before Adept, consenting small-group module trials, progressively
broader contribution opportunities, and reviewed shared responsibility at Adept.
The further clarification calls for both architect peer review and community
review of changes proposed for common adoption. These are Human product directions;
the workflow and policy details below are proposals, not ratified permissions.
No exact message timestamp or separately signed decision record is asserted.

The phone is the intended home. Ordinary participation should need only Arcanum,
with Rust shared contracts and a Kotlin Android host. Termux and development
providers are construction tools; optional integrations remain possible. See the
[implementation map](authority-and-implementation-map.md) for today's dependencies.

The controlling [Vitae authority doctrine](../doctrine/authority.md) allows entrusted
responsibility while forbidding a worth hierarchy or gating basic participation.
Accordingly, creative access means freedom to design across domains. It does not
mean unilateral control of another node, common funds, releases or constitutional
rules. Completing a curriculum, recognition of lived maturity, eligibility,
appointment and authorization for a particular effect remain distinct.

## One participant journey, separately evidenced states

| Stage | Intended participant experience | Boundary and current evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Install and activate locally | Establish protected custody and use the local node, including offline use. | Local initialization is not network registration or validator admission. Native custody exists; the whole packaged journey does not. |
| Choose network participation | Understand what connects, what data leaves, and which services require connectivity. | Show connecting, unavailable and observed connection states. Account activation alone must not fabricate chain inclusion. Peer networking and account settlement require their own verified contracts. |
| Learn and create | Use Hope privately, explore Vitae, and draft tools, curricula and modules in any domain. | Basic participation and private experimentation do not require Adept. Hope disclosure remains separately selected and consented; practice metrics cannot grant authority. |
| Join an eligible contribution program | See real opportunities, terms, evidence requirements and available funding. | No guaranteed MANA for installing, reaching a grade or being recognized. A reward requires an authorized funded program and factual contribution evidence under the Economic Constitution. |
| Try a module with a consenting group | Share an identified version; each participant chooses whether to install or try it. | Group adoption is limited to that group and declared effects. Network rules, third-party data, Treasury and monetary issuance cannot be overridden by a group vote. |
| Take on reviewed responsibilities | Request an explicit domain and scope; see review, appointment, limits and appeal status. | Recognition may support eligibility. It cannot supply missing approval or execution authority. |
| Adept / architect contribution | Design across the system, prepare larger changes, and participate in qualified peer review. | Broad creative scope plus architect review and community review precede common adoption. A title supplies no universal signing credential. |

An inactive network or unavailable funding program must leave local reflection,
learning, drafting and custody useful. Whether the product presents network setup
within account activation is a UX choice; its distinct technical states must remain
visible and truthful.

## Contribution scope is separate from grade

The following are proposed workflow scopes, not new constitutional layers or titles:

1. **Private experiment:** a draft or isolated local simulation, with no external
   effect. Creation spans economic tools, ARCnet tools and proposed Vitae classes.
2. **Consenting group trial:** an opt-in module with declared data access, effects,
   compatibility, version, removal and recovery behavior. A small audience does not
   make fund custody, network access or unsafe code low impact.
3. **Shared module proposal:** a tested candidate enters the existing authorized
   stewardship and adoption process. Earlier-grade contributors can originate it;
   reviewers and executors supply the separately required authority.
4. **System-wide proposal:** cross-domain architect work receives appropriate peer
   scrutiny and community review, followed by the applicable governance decision,
   bounded execution and verification. Constitutional changes retain stronger gates.

For example, a financial-tool experiment can simulate transfers using synthetic
balances. A trial that moves real funds needs the actual account holder's consent
and the applicable monetary contract. A drafted Vitae class is not automatically
recognized curriculum; a networking experiment cannot enlist unrelated nodes.

an exhaustive permission matrix. Everyone retains basic participation and creative
drafting; increasing responsibility concerns effects on shared systems and others.

There is a real reconciliation needed: the older permission draft places even
proposal drafting and cosmetic authoring behind V2 or a Pre-Genesis exception.
The Human's October 7 direction opens private creation earlier. Proposed correction:
separate universal private drafting and opt-in trials from formal sponsorship,
canonical adoption and shared execution. Preserve the older draft as provenance
until its exact policy amendment is reviewed; do not silently enforce either draft
as a runtime access list.

## Review and adoption proposal

Each candidate identifies its author, source version, intended audience, domain,
declared capabilities, affected data/funds, tests, known limits and recovery plan.
Review records should separately represent:

- **Architect or qualified domain review:** correctness, compatibility, security,
  doctrine boundaries and reproducible evidence, with conflicts disclosed.
- **Community review:** understandable benefits and costs, accessibility, affected
  participants' concerns, alternatives and recorded responses to objections.
- **Adoption decision:** the authorized body and applicable threshold, scope and
  conditions. Neither a popularity count nor technical approval substitutes for it.
- **Execution and observation:** approval is followed by a bounded actual action,
  factual result and recovery state; missing receipts remain unknown, not success.

Community review must be available to ordinary participants. Its exact binding
decision process remains to be designed; this proposal invents no electorate,
quorum, veto, timelock or weighting formula. Account/grade status must not obscure
conflicts, turn reviewers into owners, or remove affected participants' consent.

## Decisions still needed and bounded sequence

Already clarified by the Human: private cross-domain creativity before Adept,
consenting group experimentation, broader reviewed architect responsibility at
Adept, and peer plus community review for common adoption. Do not ask for these
preferences again merely because detailed implementation is pending.

The next policy proposal must make these remaining decisions concrete:

1. Resolve Grade X versus the post-Grade-X threshold and define recognition/review
   evidence, reviewers, appeal and privacy without compulsory private disclosure.
2. Specify domain-specific appointment and execution scopes, group-trial limits,
   suspension and recovery, and the binding relationship of community and peer review.
3. Select the specialization catalog and identify the curriculum that is ready for
   ratification, separately from unfinished content and historical metaphors.
4. Before live rewards or financial tools, define eligible funded programs and the
   outstanding monetary parameters under the Economic Constitution; no reward rate
   or automatic grade entitlement is proposed here.

Complete this blueprint/reconciliation before opening A19. A19 itself remains the
bounded protected Hope collection contract: migration, chronological recall,
deletion and corruption behavior. Broad governance, networking, curriculum delivery,
Hope model access and economic activation are separate work. Their complete
implementation is not an invented prerequisite for A19.

Suggested later verification: a fresh phone works locally without Termux/providers;
offline activation never reports network admission; a private draft acquires no
shared authority; group installation names exact capabilities and can be removed;
review approval cannot itself move funds or deploy; unknown execution outcomes are
reconciled before retry; revoked authority blocks future effects without erasing
recognition history. These are proposed acceptance cases, not passed tests.
