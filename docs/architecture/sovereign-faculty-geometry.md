# Sovereign Faculty Layer and Internal Geometry Hypotheses

## Purpose

This document preserves a design intuition that emerged after the first three Arcanum edge studies.

The first studies provisionally described:

- Hope as the Arcanum-facing port toward Nexus;
- Tempus as the Arcanum-facing port toward Commercium;
- Vitae as the Arcanum-facing port toward Theatrum.

That correspondence was useful for discovery, but it may be too restrictive as architecture.

The stronger working hypothesis is:

> **Hope, Tempus, and Vitae are sovereign faculties nested around Identity. An ARCnet system or edge may request a bounded projection from one, two, or all three faculties when the participant explicitly authorizes that use.**

Arcanum may remain the primary application/lens through which the participant directly develops and integrates those faculties without necessarily being their exclusive architectural owner.

This remains a design candidate. It does not amend the canonical module registry.

## From one-edge-one-module to multi-faculty context

The earlier simplified picture was:

```text
Hope   -> Nexus
Tempus -> Commercium
Vitae  -> Theatrum
```

This remains useful as a statement of **dominant correspondence**, but it should not become a hard exclusivity rule.

A richer model is:

```text
                         Identity / Self
                              o
                       /      |      \
                    Hope    Tempus    Vitae
                     \        |        /
                      \       |       /
                    bounded faculty projections
                              |
                        ARCnet Edge
                              |
                  destination system / lens
```

Examples:

- a Nexus action may use Hope for reflective framing, Tempus for event timing, and Vitae for explicitly selected practice context;
- a Commercium action may use Hope for maker intention, Tempus for temporal/correspondence context, and Vitae for explicitly selected craft-learning provenance;
- a Theatrum action may use Hope for meaning, Tempus for release or ritual context, and Vitae for the developmental source of an expression.

No destination receives ambient access to all three.

Each faculty projection remains separately scoped, purpose-bound, inspectable, and revocable where technically possible.

## Candidate sovereignty rule

A system should not be able to ask:

```text
Give me Hope.
Give me Tempus.
Give me Vitae.
```

It should instead request something narrow such as:

```text
HopeProjection
- selected reflection excerpt
- purpose: creator statement
- retention: destination artifact only

TempusProjection
- selected astronomical window
- purpose: event scheduling context

VitaeProjection
- selected practice receipt
- purpose: factual provenance reference
```

The participant should understand what is being projected and why.

Candidate law:

> **Faculties may inform an edge without becoming owned by the edge or destination system.**

## Arcanum as primary lens, not necessarily exclusive owner

The current canonical architecture treats Hope, Tempus, and Vitae as Arcanum modules. That remains controlling until amended.

The Creation Era hypothesis being investigated is more subtle:

```text
Identity / Self
      |
Hope - Tempus - Vitae
      |
   Arcanum
primary self-development lens
```

Under this hypothesis:

- Arcanum is where the participant most directly reflects, orients, practices, learns, and understands becoming;
- the faculties remain identity-centered enough that other ARCnet systems can receive narrowly authorized projections;
- Nexus, Commercium, Theatrum, Aerarium, Imperium, Protection, and Architect do not need to duplicate these faculties as separate private dossiers;
- the runtime may eventually need a shared faculty-context boundary below application presentation.

This is an architecture question for a future Module Architecture v2 review.

## Nested Platonic-solid intuition

A new symbolic/structural hypothesis emerged:

```text
Hope   <-> octahedron
Tempus <-> dodecahedron
Vitae  <-> icosahedron
```

These correspondences are **design hypotheses**, not claims that Platonic solids scientifically determine psychology, time, or human development.

Their value must be tested by whether they create coherent information architecture and usable interfaces.

### Hope and the octahedron

The Hope/octahedron intuition is geometrically notable because the common interior of two interpenetrating tetrahedra in the standard stella octangula is a **regular octahedron**.

The six octahedral vertices occur where a tetrahedral edge from one tetrahedron crosses a tetrahedral edge from the other on each face of the surrounding cube.

This gives a mathematically real inner structure:

```text
2 tetrahedra
    |
interpenetrate
    v
shared interior = octahedron
    |
6 inner vertices
```

This does **not** prove that Hope should use the octahedron.

It does make the hypothesis unusually worth investigating because Hope has already begun to look less like one outward edge and more like an integrative, self-facing faculty.

Possible semantic reading to test:

> Hope may describe the reflective interior where otherwise distinct system relationships can be brought into conscious relation without granting Hope authority over those systems.

### Tempus and the dodecahedron

The dodecahedron has:

- 12 pentagonal faces;
- 20 vertices;
- 30 edges.

Its twelve faces make it a natural candidate for a twelvefold temporal/correspondence presentation such as zodiacal, calendrical, seasonal, or other explicitly labeled cultural/astronomical indexing.

Tempus canon requires observable astronomical grounding and metaphysical neutrality. Therefore any zodiacal or symbolic use must remain optional, interpretive, and clearly separated from astronomical fact.

The dodecahedron should be investigated as an **information organization geometry**, not as causal cosmology.

### Vitae and the icosahedron

The icosahedron has:

- 20 triangular faces;
- 12 vertices;
- 30 edges.

It is the geometric dual of the dodecahedron.

That duality may be useful for Vitae and Tempus because:

- dodecahedron faces correspond to icosahedron vertices;
- dodecahedron vertices correspond to icosahedron faces;
- both contain 30 edges.

A possible future research question is whether temporal contexts and developmental pathways can be represented as dual structures without reducing becoming to a score or deterministic schedule.

No such mapping is ratified yet.

## Layered geometry hypothesis

The resulting visual/information model could eventually contain several nested, independently meaningful layers:

```text
                 ARCnet / Merkaba field
            two interpenetrating tetrahedra

                     outer systems
                          |
             -------------------------
                          |
                   Hope octahedron
                          |
              Tempus dodecahedron
                          |
               Vitae icosahedron
                          |
                    Identity / Self
```

The exact nesting order shown above is illustrative only. The solids may overlap, interpenetrate, or operate as switchable coordinate systems rather than literal concentric shells.

A future UI could use controlled rotation/orientation to express:

- which ARCnet system lens is active;
- which Hope relation is being reflected upon;
- where the participant is within a selected Tempus cycle;
- which Vitae path or practice context is being explored.

Rotation should be understood as a **lens/state transformation**, not as authority or automatic progression.

Accessibility, reduced motion, and ordinary mobile usability remain mandatory constraints.

## Six inner stella-octangula junctions

The standard stella octangula supplies six mathematically distinct internal edge-crossing points.

Using the cube representation:

- the first tetrahedron contributes six face-diagonal edges;
- the second tetrahedron contributes the opposite six face-diagonal edges;
- on each of the cube's six faces, one edge from each tetrahedron crosses at the face center;
- those six face centers are the six vertices of the shared inner octahedron.

Therefore a real six-junction structure exists without inventing extra geometry.

Candidate interpretation:

> **An inner junction may represent a place where one lived/generative relationship and one stewardship/system relationship can be composed or compared.**

However, no semantic mapping should be assigned until the eight outer systems receive a fixed coordinate assignment.

The junction should initially be treated as:

```text
intersection / composition point
not an application
not a new sovereign actor
not a new authority source
```

This extends the Edge Contract hypothesis:

```text
edge = primitive bilateral relationship
inner junction = possible dual-edge composition point
workflow = explicit composition across one or more contracts
```

## Point-count clarification: stella octangula versus Fruit of Life

A count distinction must be preserved so symbolic systems are not accidentally presented as the same geometry.

### Current stella-octangula topology

If we count the current working topology geometrically:

```text
8 outer stella-octangula / cube vertices
+ 6 inner octahedral vertices
+ 1 center
= 15 notable positions
```

The current eight outer vertices are the proposed ARCnet systems.

The six inner points are candidate junctions, not yet systems.

The center is Human / Identity.

### Fruit of Life

In the common modern sacred-geometry convention, the **Fruit of Life** is represented by **13 circles**:

```text
1 center
+ 12 surrounding circles
= 13 circles
```

Therefore the current stella-octangula point count does **not** automatically equal the Fruit-of-Life count.

The intuition of **Self + 12 informational positions** is still worth investigating, especially because:

- the dodecahedron provides 12 faces;
- the icosahedron provides 12 vertices;
- many temporal/cultural systems use twelvefold indexing.

But the project should not claim that the present eight-system Merkaba plus six inner junctions already *is* a 13-node Fruit-of-Life architecture.

Candidate research question:

> Does the Fruit-of-Life 1+12 structure describe a different information layer nested within or projected through the eight-system topology rather than the literal vertex count of the Merkaba?

## Information systems versus information positions

The phrase **information system** should be used carefully.

The current working model distinguishes:

- **system** — a major ARCnet application/domain such as Nexus or Commercium;
- **faculty** — an identity-centered interpretive/temporal/developmental capacity such as Hope, Tempus, or Vitae;
- **edge** — a typed bilateral relationship between systems;
- **junction** — a candidate composition point where relationships intersect;
- **coordinate / informational position** — a location in a symbolic information geometry that may not correspond to a separately executable application.

This vocabulary can prevent the geometry from generating unnecessary software merely to satisfy a point count.

## Edge Contract implication

The ARCnet Edge Contract should evolve from assuming one primary component/port per endpoint to allowing **zero or more sovereign faculty projections**.

Candidate structure:

```text
EDGE INVOCATION
- origin system
- destination system
- action
- actor / authority

FACULTY PROJECTIONS (optional)
- Hope projection
- Tempus projection
- Vitae projection

for each projection
- selected fields
- purpose
- destination
- retention
- audience
- provenance
- revocation behavior

then
- destination-native action
- economic consequence
- receipt
- optional settlement
```

A faculty projection must never be interpreted as permission to access the entire faculty history.

## Candidate invariants

1. **Faculty Non-Exclusivity** — a faculty may participate in multiple edges; one edge does not own one faculty.
2. **Faculty Sovereignty** — a destination receives a bounded projection, not ambient access to the faculty store.
3. **Context Composition** — one invocation may combine multiple separately scoped faculty projections.
4. **Purpose Binding** — faculty context approved for one purpose may not silently be reused for another.
5. **Lens Independence** — changing ARCnet system lens does not change the sovereign identity or grant new faculty access.
6. **Junction Non-Authority** — a geometric intersection creates no permission or institutional authority by itself.
7. **Geometry Non-Determinism** — geometric representation may organize information but may not claim causal control over a person.
8. **No Numerology-Driven Software** — system count and module count must follow legitimate function, not the desire to fill a symbolic number.

## Questions to test in the next edge studies

The three remaining base edges of the generative tetrahedron should now be studied with this broader model:

- Nexus ↔ Commercium;
- Nexus ↔ Theatrum;
- Commercium ↔ Theatrum.

For each edge, ask:

- Does Hope have a legitimate optional projection?
- Does Tempus have a legitimate optional projection?
- Does Vitae have a legitimate optional projection?
- Can those projections remain participant-controlled rather than becoming destination-owned profiles?
- Does the edge remain meaningful even when no faculty context is supplied?

If all three base edges naturally use the faculty triad without requiring exclusive ownership, the identity-centered faculty-layer hypothesis becomes substantially stronger.

## Next gate

Complete the six edges of the generative tetrahedron and compare them as one family.

Only after that comparison should the project decide whether the earlier one-edge-one-faculty pattern was merely a discovery aid or a true architectural constraint.
