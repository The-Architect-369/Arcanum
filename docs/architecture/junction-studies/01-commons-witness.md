# Inner Octahedral Junction Study 01 — Commons Witness

## Crossing under test

```text
Arcanum ↔ Nexus
       ×
Protection ↔ Aerarium
```

The first edge concerns **interiority/reflection ↔ relation/expression**.

The second concerns **custody/integrity assurance ↔ shared-resource/Treasury execution**.

The question is whether their geometric crossing exposes a real coordination problem without inventing a ninth authority surface.

## Candidate shared problem

Public or shared resources need to be understandable to human beings and communities without turning social opinion into spending authority or financial audit into personal surveillance.

At the same time, participants need a way to:

- inspect bounded public-resource evidence;
- understand what happened;
- discuss it socially;
- reflect on its meaning or consequences;
- raise a concern;

without gaining the ability to move Treasury resources merely because they saw, discussed, or interpreted the evidence.

This suggests a candidate junction function:

> **Commons Witness — a human-readable, non-executing bridge between protected shared-resource evidence and sovereign/social understanding.**

The name is provisional.

## Candidate composition

```text
Protection / Aerarium
bounded public-resource evidence
        |
        v
Commons Witness Artifact
        |
        +--> Nexus discussion / public explanation
        |
        +--> Arcanum private reflection / understanding
```

Reverse passage may produce:

```text
Arcanum / Nexus concern or observation
        |
        v
Commons Concern Artifact
        |
        v
Protection / Aerarium review queue
```

The concern is evidence/input, not a spending instruction.

## Candidate artifact

A `CommonsWitnessArtifact` could expose only bounded factual state such as:

```text
resource / lane
amount or bounded value summary
purpose
ratified authorization reference
custody / execution status
relevant verification state
time / finality status
public receipt references
human-readable explanation
```

It should not expose:

- unrelated personal wallet activity;
- private Hope reflections;
- private Vitae history;
- hidden security details that create new attack surface;
- complete custodian personal dossiers.

## Core distinctions

```text
visibility != authority
conversation != authorization
concern != guilt
public audit != personal surveillance
reflection != governance outcome
witnessing a transfer != controlling the transfer
```

## Why the junction may be real

The two edge studies independently produced a similar requirement from opposite directions:

- Arcanum ↔ Nexus requires controlled movement from protected/private meaning into social visibility.
- Protection ↔ Aerarium requires controlled movement from sensitive custody/integrity state into public auditability.

Both therefore need **bounded disclosure that increases understanding without increasing authority**.

That is a genuine shared coordination function rather than a symbolic resemblance.

## Candidate law — Witness Without Control

> **ARCnet may make consequential shared-resource actions intelligible and discussable without granting execution authority to the surfaces that witness them.**

This is narrower than governance transparency. It is an information law about how high-consequence state becomes human-readable.

## Faculty projection test

### Hope

Hope may help a participant privately interpret what a public-resource event means to them.

That reflection stays private unless deliberately shared.

Hope must not infer trustworthiness, guilt, or governance eligibility from the participant's reaction.

### Tempus

Operational chronology is useful:

- proposal time;
- time-lock state;
- execution time;
- finality;
- audit age.

Symbolic Tempus overlays should not alter financial or governance facts.

### Vitae

No Vitae projection is required merely to inspect or discuss public-resource evidence.

Where a later review role requires eligibility, that belongs to the separate authority process rather than the witness artifact itself.

## Candidate UI expression

A human-facing card might say:

```text
COMMONS WITNESS

Purpose: Security audit grant
Authorized amount: ...
Authorization: Imperium receipt ...
Treasury state: executed / finalized
Protection state: verification available

[Understand]
[Discuss in Nexus]
[Raise a concern]
[View receipts]
```

None of these controls should imply a spend action.

## Failure conditions

Reject or redesign this junction if it becomes:

- a popularity vote over Treasury facts;
- a social-credit surface;
- a way for Nexus engagement to alter custody;
- a public dump of sensitive security details;
- a psychological profiling surface for people who question resource actions;
- a hidden governance pathway.

## Preliminary result

**Meaningful, but informational rather than executive.**

This is the weakest-looking crossing at first glance, yet it becomes coherent when treated as a **witness/transparency junction** rather than a new decision system.

Its usefulness depends on maintaining strict non-execution.
