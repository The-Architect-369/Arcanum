# Inner Octahedral Junction Study 02 — Practice Safety

## Crossing under test

```text
Arcanum ↔ Commercium
       ×
Protection ↔ Imperium
```

The generative edge concerns **personal temporal/symbolic context ↔ physical craft and exchange**.
The stewardship edge concerns **security/risk evidence ↔ governance policy and authorization**.

## Candidate shared problem

Physical practices, crafted goods, services, and claims can create real safety, legal, fraud, and public-risk concerns. Yet symbolic or spiritual language must not automatically become a governance target merely because it is non-material or interpretive.

This suggests a junction centered on distinguishing:

```text
personal meaning
from
physical claim
from
safety evidence
from
policy response
```

Candidate name:

> **Practice Safety** — a bounded coordination point where physical-world claims and practices can be reviewed for actual safety/policy consequences without regulating private meaning as such.

## Candidate flow

```text
Arcanum / Commercium craft or service claim
        |
        v
Practice Safety Review Artifact
        |
        +--> Protection evidence review
        |
        +--> Imperium policy applicability check
```

Possible outputs:

```text
no stewardship action required
label / disclosure required
independent verification required
restricted physical claim
policy review required
safety incident review
```

## Core distinctions

```text
symbolic meaning != regulated factual claim
maker intention != verified efficacy
risk evidence != ideological judgment
safety policy != metaphysical doctrine
policy applicability != confiscation authority
```

## Why the junction is real

Arcanum ↔ Commercium already exposed the Semantic Attestation Boundary and evidence grades.
Protection ↔ Imperium already exposed the separation between technical evidence and policy authority.

Their crossing naturally creates a shared need: **typed claim review**.

## Candidate law — Claim-Type Before Policy

> **ARCnet should classify what kind of claim is being made before applying safety, verification, or governance machinery to it.**

A maker saying “I prepared this according to my ritual practice” is different from saying “this object medically cures disease.”

## Faculty posture

Hope may provide maker-authored meaning only by explicit choice. Tempus may provide factual timing evidence. Vitae should not determine whether a product is safe or whether a claim is true.

## Failure conditions

Reject or redesign if this junction becomes a morality police, belief classifier, automatic censorship layer, or reputation score.

## Preliminary result

**Strongly meaningful.**

This is one of the clearer junctions because both source edges independently require typed claims, evidence boundaries, and separation of interpretation from enforceable fact.
