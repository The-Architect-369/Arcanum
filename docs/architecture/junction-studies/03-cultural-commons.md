# Inner Octahedral Junction Study 03 — Cultural Commons

## Crossing under test

```text
Arcanum ↔ Theatrum
       ×
Imperium ↔ Aerarium
```

The generative edge concerns **becoming/practice ↔ symbolic and digital expression**.
The stewardship edge concerns **collective authorization ↔ public-resource execution**.

## Candidate shared problem

A community may choose to support cultural, educational, artistic, ritual, archival, or symbolic work as a public good. But public funding must not define personal becoming, dictate artistic meaning, or convert funded expression into doctrine.

Candidate name:

> **Cultural Commons** — a coordination point for supporting shared cultural expression while preserving creator sovereignty and bounded public-resource authority.

## Candidate composition

```text
Arcanum / Theatrum expression or cultural work
        |
        v
Public-Goods Proposal Artifact
        |
        +--> Imperium deliberation / authorization
        |
        +--> Aerarium bounded funding / accounting
```

Outputs may include:

```text
unfunded private work
community-supported cultural artifact
bounded public grant
archive/publication support
commission with explicit scope
```

## Core distinctions

```text
funding != authorship
patronage != doctrine
public support != ownership of creator identity
collective value != human worth
grant approval != artistic truth
Treasury support != forced publication
```

## Why the junction is real

Arcanum ↔ Theatrum already requires separation between lived recognition and representation.
Imperium ↔ Aerarium already requires separation between collective authorization and Treasury execution.

Their crossing naturally creates a public-goods interface where a work can be proposed for collective support without collapsing creator, governance, and Treasury roles.

## Candidate law — Patronage Without Capture

> **Shared resources may support expression without purchasing control over the creator's identity, becoming, or private source state beyond the explicit terms of the support.**

## Faculty posture

Hope can support a creator statement if voluntarily included. Tempus can provide deadlines, release windows, or historical context. Vitae may provide selected factual practice provenance but must not become a grant-worthiness score.

## Failure conditions

Reject or redesign if this junction becomes a state-art ranking system, prestige ladder, pay-to-recognition mechanism, ideological funding filter, or automatic public ownership of privately created source material.

## Preliminary result

**Strongly meaningful.**

The crossing produces a coherent concept: a cultural/public-goods funding interface where the separation-of-powers rules remain intact.
