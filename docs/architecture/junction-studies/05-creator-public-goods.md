# Inner Octahedral Junction Study 05 — Creator Public Goods

## Crossing under test

```text
Nexus ↔ Theatrum
       ×
Architect ↔ Aerarium
```

The generative edge concerns **social relation / audience ↔ digital creation / publication**.
The stewardship edge concerns **system work / public-goods design ↔ funding / shared-resource stewardship**.

## Candidate shared problem

Creators and communities need shared tools, publishing infrastructure, archives, accessibility systems, moderation tooling, and cultural utilities. These may deserve common funding, but popularity must not automatically become funding authority and funding must not purchase creator identity or cultural dominance.

Candidate name:

> **Creator Public Goods** — a coordination point for proposing, building, and funding shared creative/social infrastructure without collapsing audience, builder, and Treasury roles.

## Candidate composition

```text
Nexus / Theatrum need or cultural opportunity
        |
        v
Creator-Infrastructure Proposal
        |
        +--> Architect design / implementation candidate
        |
        +--> Aerarium funding / accounting path
```

Examples include:

- public media storage infrastructure;
- accessible publishing tools;
- creator collaboration utilities;
- archival infrastructure;
- community-owned exhibition surfaces;
- open cultural tooling.

## Core distinctions

```text
popularity != funding authority
funding != creator rank
builder control != cultural ownership
public infrastructure != compulsory public content
audience size != public-good merit
```

## Why the junction is real

Nexus ↔ Theatrum needs a boundary between creation and publication/audience.
Architect ↔ Aerarium needs a boundary between technical work and public-resource funding.

The crossing naturally describes **shared creator/community infrastructure as a public good**.

## Candidate law — Infrastructure Support Without Cultural Ranking

> **ARCnet may fund shared creative infrastructure without using popularity, prestige, or audience scale as a proxy for human or cultural worth.**

## Faculty posture

Hope may contribute voluntary qualitative need statements. Tempus may contribute project windows and operational milestones. Vitae must not become a creator funding score; bounded responsibility eligibility may apply only to stewardship roles through separate authority processes.

## Failure conditions

Reject or redesign if this becomes a popularity subsidy engine, cultural prestige ladder, permanent builder monopoly, or indirect mechanism for Treasury control of expression.

## Preliminary result

**Strongly meaningful.**

The junction produces a practical public-goods function distinct from direct patronage of individual works: it supports the infrastructure that allows communities and creators to make and share.
