# ARCnet Edge Study 23 — Theatrum ↔ Imperium

## Candidate meaning
> **Culture ↔ Commons Rules**

Theatrum produces art, symbolic expression, digital environments, personas, and cultural artifacts. Imperium may govern only those public rules actually delegated to it: rights, commons standards, safety procedures, licensing frameworks, and shared-space policy.

## Strongest law — Governance May Bound the Commons; It Must Not Become the Author of Culture
> **Imperium may establish legitimate public rules around shared cultural spaces without deciding the meaning, value, or worth of expression merely by vote.**

## Candidate laws
1. **Expression–Ratification Separation** — cultural meaning does not require governance approval merely to exist.
2. **Rights–Taste Separation** — public rules should govern rights/safety/process rather than aesthetic preference.
3. **No Ideological Popularity Sovereignty** — majority taste cannot become doctrine by default.
4. **Prospective Rule Integrity** — new commons rules declare scope and effective time.
5. **Cultural Provenance Without Identity Dossiers** — authorship/rights evidence remains bounded.

## Orientation result
Meaningful operational edge. Theatrum ↔ Imperium governs the shared conditions of culture; it does not rival the deeper Theatrum ↔ Protection mirror of expression ↔ integrity/provenance.
