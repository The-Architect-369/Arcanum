# ARCnet Edge Study 20 — Commercium ↔ Imperium

## Candidate meaning

> **Exchange ↔ Rules**

Commercium enables voluntary exchange, craft, services, pricing, fulfillment, and market coordination. Imperium may define or ratify those bounded public rules that governance is actually authorized to control.

## Strongest law — Regulation Must Bind Rules, Not Human Worth

> **Imperium may govern public market rules within delegated authority; it may not convert economic behavior into a generalized moral, identity, or worth score.**

## Candidate flow

```text
Commercium market condition / dispute class / rule request
        |
        | bounded evidence
        v
Imperium policy / proposal path
        |
        | higher-law checks
        v
ratified market rule
        |
        | explicit version / scope
        v
Commercium implementation
```

## Candidate laws

1. **Rule–Transaction Separation** — a rule defines permissible conditions; it does not rewrite a finalized transaction secretly.
2. **Economic Privacy** — governance should receive the minimum market evidence needed for legitimate public rules.
3. **No Wealth-to-Rule Shortcut** — capital cannot purchase constitutional or governance supremacy.
4. **Prospective Rule Integrity** — new rules should declare effective scope/time rather than fabricating past noncompliance.
5. **Dispute–Identity Separation** — a commercial dispute concerns an event/contract, not the essence or worth of a participant.

## Orientation result

This is one of the strongest operational edges and explains why it was tempting as a mirror candidate. Yet it remains directional: market activity is governed by public rules. The deeper mirror **Commercium ↔ Aerarium** still captures the dual circulation of private exchange and common resources more symmetrically.
