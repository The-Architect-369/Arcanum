# Stewardship Tetrahedron Synthesis — Edge Studies 07–12

## Purpose

This document compares the six first-pass edge studies among the candidate stewardship systems:

- **Architect** — system design, implementation candidates, builder coordination;
- **Protection** — security, provenance, integrity, verification, bounded safety enforcement;
- **Imperium** — formal governance, collective authorization, amendment and review;
- **Aerarium** — public/shared-resource interface over Treasury/economic stewardship and execution.

The purpose is not to force a geometric symmetry. It is to identify patterns that survived six materially different stewardship relationships and to expose where the candidate topology still lacks evidence.

All conclusions remain design-candidate.

## Six stewardship edges

### 07 — Architect ↔ Protection

Candidate meaning:

> **Build / Change ↔ Verification / Assurance**

Primary distinctions:

- builder evidence ≠ independent verification;
- security finding ≠ authority to legislate;
- remediation ≠ silent reviewer mutation;
- signed ≠ safe ≠ trusted ≠ approved ≠ installed;
- capability expansion requires explicit review.

### 08 — Architect ↔ Imperium

Candidate meaning:

> **Design / Proposal ↔ Ratification / Collective Decision**

Primary distinctions:

- proposal authorship ≠ decision authority;
- valid process ≠ authority forbidden by higher law;
- ratification ≠ execution;
- technical evidence ≠ collective value judgment;
- the voted object must be bound to an exact proposal version/digest.

### 09 — Architect ↔ Aerarium

Candidate meaning:

> **Work / Public-Goods Design ↔ Funding / Shared-Resource Stewardship**

Primary distinctions:

- funding ≠ architecture authority;
- approved budget ≠ disbursement;
- deliverable evidence ≠ human-worth score;
- technical need ≠ spending authority;
- Treasury transfer ≠ MANA issuance.

### 10 — Protection ↔ Imperium

Candidate meaning:

> **Risk / Security Evidence ↔ Policy / Governance Authorization**

Primary distinctions:

- threat evidence ≠ unlimited response authority;
- governance preference ≠ technical truth;
- emergency speed should derive from preauthorized bounded interlocks;
- emergency authority needs sunset/review;
- transparency ≠ indiscriminate publication of exploitable detail.

### 11 — Protection ↔ Aerarium

Candidate meaning:

> **Custody / Integrity Assurance ↔ Shared-Resource Stewardship / Treasury Execution**

Primary distinctions:

- custody control ≠ ownership;
- security may prevent unauthorized spending but may not invent a replacement spend;
- compromised key ≠ compromised human identity;
- anomaly ≠ guilt;
- Treasury auditability ≠ universal personal financial surveillance.

### 12 — Imperium ↔ Aerarium

Candidate meaning:

> **Collective Decision / Authorization ↔ Treasury Execution / Public Resource Stewardship**

Primary distinctions:

- governance approval ≠ Treasury execution;
- a vote cannot create Treasury authority forbidden by higher law;
- execution must match recipient, amount, purpose, and conditions actually ratified;
- mandate ≠ custody;
- approved ≠ queued ≠ executed ≠ finalized.

## The tetrahedron as a separation-of-powers system

Across all six studies, the four candidate stewardship systems are becoming legible as four different kinds of responsibility:

```text
Architect
    designs / proposes / implements candidates

Protection
    verifies / detects / enforces already-authorized safety boundaries

Imperium
    deliberates / ratifies / delegates bounded collective authority

Aerarium
    presents / accounts for / routes / executes shared-resource authority
    through underlying Treasury and economic law
```

A compact form is:

```text
DESIGN      → Architect
VERIFY      → Protection
AUTHORIZE   → Imperium
STEWARD / EXECUTE RESOURCES → Aerarium
```

This is not a strict sequential pipeline for every action. It is a **separation-of-powers grammar**.

Candidate constitutional design law:

> **No stewardship point should become sufficient by itself for high-impact system action.**

A high-impact action may require different combinations of:

- a coherent design;
- verification/security evidence;
- legitimate authorization;
- bounded custody/execution.

The exact required combination depends on the domain.

## Strong recurring laws across all six stewardship edges

### 1. Authority Conservation

Crossing a stewardship edge does not increase authority merely because another system accepted the object.

Examples:

- a successful security review is not governance approval;
- a governance outcome is not completed execution;
- a funded project is not architecturally correct because it was funded;
- a signer is not the source of the public mandate.

### 2. Typed State and Typed Claim Law

Stewardship requires unusually precise state language.

The studies repeatedly distinguished pairs such as:

```text
finding / policy
proposal / ratification
budget / disbursement
authorization / execution
signature / trust
anomaly / guilt
approved / finalized
custody / ownership
```

Candidate law:

> **High-authority interfaces must encode the exact type of claim and stage of action rather than relying on vague words such as approved, safe, verified, or authorized.**

### 3. Non-Transitive Authority

Authority granted on one relationship does not automatically cross another.

Examples:

- Protection approval does not authorize deployment;
- Imperium ratification does not authorize arbitrary Treasury changes;
- Aerarium funding does not authorize a broader architecture change;
- Architect technical necessity does not authorize emergency policy.

### 4. Provenance Without Surveillance

All six edges require strong auditability, but none require complete personal dossiers.

Stewardship provenance should answer:

- what artifact/action was involved;
- who/what authority authorized it;
- which version/policy applied;
- what evidence supported it;
- what happened next.

It should not default to private Hope history, full Vitae history, unrelated financial activity, ideology, or psychological inference.

### 5. Independent Evidence

The stewardship side repeatedly needs evidence produced or checked by a role distinct from the actor whose interests are affected.

This does not imply bureaucratic duplication for every low-risk action.

It does imply that high-impact systems should avoid structures where:

```text
I propose it
I approve it
I verify it
I fund it
I execute it
I audit myself
```

all collapse into one unreviewable authority.

## Four role-boundary laws

The six studies jointly expose four particularly strong laws.

### Architect Law — Authorship does not confer authority

Architect can make coherent proposals and implementations.

It does not ratify merely because it understands or authored them.

### Protection Law — Verification does not confer sovereignty

Protection may discover risk and enforce already-authorized controls.

It should not silently invent constitutional or policy authority.

### Imperium Law — Process does not manufacture higher authority

Governance may exercise authority legitimately delegated to it.

A vote cannot make an action constitutionally valid if the authority to perform it does not exist.

### Aerarium Law — Custody/execution does not confer ownership or policy authority

Aerarium/Treasury may execute authorized resource actions.

It does not own common resources by virtue of controlling their execution and cannot create monetary authority by UI, custody, or discretion.

## Pre-Genesis interpretation

The current canonical phase matters.

During Pre-Genesis:

- the Human Architect remains primary editorial/release authority;
- governance is not assumed to be fully activated merely because Imperium is a candidate system;
- Aerarium must remain subordinate to the existing Treasury Constitution and Economic Constitution;
- Protection remains a candidate system name/topology over security functions that are currently distributed across runtime, repository, policy, and review surfaces;
- Architect remains bounded by the canonical explicit-authorization model.

The topology is therefore describing a **future separation of responsibilities**, not claiming all four applications currently exist.

## Sovereign faculty layer result

The stewardship studies strongly support the earlier conclusion that Hope, Tempus, and Vitae should not be modeled as exclusive modules assigned one-to-one to individual edges.

However, stewardship edges also reveal an important refinement:

> **Private faculty context should be minimized more aggressively as authority and institutional consequence increase.**

### Hope

Hope rarely has a legitimate default role in high-authority stewardship decisions.

Private reflection must not become:

- a security risk score;
- governance ideology score;
- grant-worthiness score;
- fraud score;
- Treasury trust score.

### Tempus

Tempus contributes mostly **factual operational time** on stewardship edges:

- proposal windows;
- time-locks;
- expiry;
- key/certificate validity;
- incident chronology;
- funding milestones.

Its symbolic/correspondence layer should not generate institutional authority.

### Vitae

Vitae may contribute bounded eligibility for responsibilities where canon expressly allows.

The stewardship studies repeatedly confirm:

```text
Vitae eligibility
    ↓
review
    ↓
authority envelope
    ↓
execution mechanism
```

Vitae does not skip those middle layers.

## Comparison with the generative tetrahedron

The two families now have distinct character.

### Generative tetrahedron

```text
Arcanum
Nexus
Commercium
Theatrum
```

Dominant concerns:

- meaning;
- expression;
- creation;
- relationship;
- physical/digital embodiment;
- voluntary sharing;
- ownership and exchange.

Its central architectural law is approximately:

> **Sovereign context may be transformed into bounded derivatives without exposing the source self.**

### Stewardship tetrahedron

```text
Architect
Protection
Imperium
Aerarium
```

Dominant concerns:

- authority;
- evidence;
- verification;
- governance;
- custody;
- public resources;
- execution and audit.

Its central architectural law is approximately:

> **High-impact power should be decomposed into distinct, bounded, auditable responsibilities rather than concentrated in one system.**

The two tetrahedra are therefore not redundant copies.

They appear to encode two different modes of ARCnet:

```text
GENERATIVE
people create, relate, embody, exchange

STEWARDSHIP
systems verify, authorize, fund, protect, execute
```

## Coordinate-derived inner-junction pairing

Now that both tetrahedral edge families have semantic studies, the six mathematically real inner octahedral vertices can be examined more rigorously.

Under the current mirror pairing:

```text
Arcanum    ↔ Architect
Nexus      ↔ Imperium
Commercium ↔ Aerarium
Theatrum   ↔ Protection
```

one consistent cube/stella-octangula coordinate assignment pairs each generative tetrahedral edge with the stewardship edge crossing it on the same cube face.

That yields the following six geometric crossing pairs:

```text
Arcanum ↔ Nexus
    crosses
Aerarium ↔ Protection

Arcanum ↔ Commercium
    crosses
Imperium ↔ Protection

Arcanum ↔ Theatrum
    crosses
Imperium ↔ Aerarium

Nexus ↔ Commercium
    crosses
Architect ↔ Protection

Nexus ↔ Theatrum
    crosses
Architect ↔ Aerarium

Commercium ↔ Theatrum
    crosses
Architect ↔ Imperium
```

This mapping follows from the geometry once the current four mirror pairs are fixed; it is not yet a semantic claim about what each inner vertex means.

That distinction is essential.

## Why the crossing pairs are promising

The pairings are nontrivial enough to test rather than simply celebrate.

They place, for example:

```text
social relation ↔ exchange
```

against:

```text
system construction ↔ security verification
```

and:

```text
physical ↔ digital embodiment
```

against:

```text
system design ↔ formal governance
```

If coherent composite meanings emerge from all six crossings without being forced, then the inner octahedron may represent a genuine **coordination/composition layer** rather than decorative geometry.

If several crossings remain semantically arbitrary, the hypothesis should be weakened or discarded.

## Junction constraints before naming them

Before assigning names or software responsibilities to an inner vertex, the following constraints should hold:

1. It must derive from the two crossing edge studies rather than symbolic preference alone.
2. It must solve a real coordination or information problem.
3. It must not create a new sovereign authority.
4. It must preserve the authority boundaries of all four systems participating through the two edges.
5. It should preferably be representable as a composed workflow or information junction rather than a new application.
6. It must remain meaningful even without sacred-geometry presentation in the UI.

## Strongest new hypothesis

After twelve edge studies, the current macro-pattern can be stated as:

> **The outer tetrahedra describe domains and bilateral relationships; the inner octahedron may describe coordination points where generative activity meets stewardship responsibility.**

This is now evidence-backed enough to investigate, but not yet to ratify.

## What remains unproven

The studies do not yet prove:

- that all 28 K8 edges should become executable contracts;
- that all six inner octahedral vertices deserve named informational roles;
- that Hope/octahedron, Tempus/dodecahedron, and Vitae/icosahedron are correct permanent mappings;
- that Aerarium is the final public-facing name;
- that the current mirror-pair assignment is final;
- that the geometry should be directly exposed in ordinary UI;
- that any proposed Creation Era topology supersedes the canonical module registry.

## Next gate

The most evidence-efficient next study is now the **six inner octahedral junctions** using the coordinate-derived crossing pairs above.

For each junction:

1. place the two already-studied edges beside each other;
2. identify any real shared object, workflow, or coordination problem;
3. determine whether the crossing produces a meaningful composition;
4. reject any junction that requires invented authority or forced symbolism;
5. record reusable composition laws separately from edge laws.

Only after this six-junction test should the project move into the twelve cross-tetrahedron/cube-edge relationships or begin formalizing a Creation Era roadmap from the geometry.
