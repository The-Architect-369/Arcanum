# ARCnet Edge Study 19 — Commercium ↔ Architect

## Candidate meaning

> **Marketplace Need ↔ Tooling and Infrastructure**

Commercium can expose missing capabilities in listings, fulfillment, provenance, payments, dispute handling, and maker tooling. Architect can turn bounded market needs into implementation candidates.

## Strongest law — Market Demand Does Not Rewrite Architecture by Purchase

> **Economic demand may inform what builders investigate; spending power does not buy architectural sovereignty or constitutional exemption.**

## Candidate laws

1. **Demand–Authority Separation** — commercial demand is evidence, not design authority.
2. **No Pay-to-Spec** — payment may commission work but cannot bypass doctrine/security/governance review.
3. **Tooling Neutrality** — Architect should build reusable capabilities where appropriate rather than privileged hidden paths for individual merchants.
4. **Market Evidence Minimization** — product needs should not require full customer financial dossiers.
5. **Implementation Provenance** — market-driven changes retain source, rationale, review, and version history.

## Orientation result

A strong operational edge: marketplace ↔ tooling. It is not mirror-like because Commercium ↔ Aerarium more deeply captures private/distributed circulation versus common-resource circulation.
