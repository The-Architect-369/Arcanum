# ARCnet Edge Study 15 — Arcanum ↔ Aerarium

## Candidate meaning

> **Human Development Need ↔ Public Support**

Arcanum may surface participant-selected needs, program ideas, educational infrastructure requests, accessibility requirements, or aggregate evidence about beneficial human-development resources. Aerarium may support shared-resource proposals and Treasury-authorized public goods.

The edge must not turn vulnerability, inner life, or developmental history into a funding score.

## Core flow

```text
Arcanum need / public-good concept
        |
        | bounded, consented derivative or aggregate
        v
Public Benefit Request
        |
        v
Aerarium funding / resource pathway
        |
        | separate governance + Treasury authority
        v
bounded support
```

## Strongest law — Fund the Capability, Not the Worth of the Person

> **Shared resources may support conditions for human development without pricing, ranking, or purchasing human becoming.**

Examples include accessibility, learning infrastructure, community spaces, privacy-preserving tools, or program grants. MANA may fund the resource; it may not buy Vitae recognition or human worth.

## Candidate laws

1. **Need–Worth Separation** — requesting support does not lower or raise human value.
2. **Aggregate Before Intimate Disclosure** — public-goods evidence should prefer aggregates and bounded claims over private reflection histories.
3. **No Vulnerability Auction** — funding must not reward increasingly intimate disclosure.
4. **Support–Recognition Separation** — receiving public support cannot purchase or imply Vitae advancement.
5. **Purpose-Bound Funding** — resources are tied to the ratified public-good purpose, not to control over the participant's inner life.

## Orientation result

Arcanum ↔ Aerarium is meaningful as **public support for human-development infrastructure**, but it is operational rather than mirror-like. The relationship is resource support, not a deep same-axis duality.
