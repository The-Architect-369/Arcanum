# ARCnet Edge Study 13 — Arcanum ↔ Protection

## Candidate meaning

> **Interior Sovereignty ↔ Protective Boundary**

Arcanum holds high-context personal reflection, temporal orientation, practice, and becoming. Protection exists to protect the container, capabilities, provenance, recovery paths, and authorized disclosure around that material.

The edge is meaningful only if Protection can secure Arcanum **without becoming an interpreter or judge of the interior self**.

## Core flow

```text
Arcanum private state / intended action
        |
        | narrow protection request
        v
Protection boundary check
        |
        +--> allow within declared scope
        +--> deny capability violation
        +--> request explicit reauthorization
        +--> provide recovery / integrity evidence
```

Protection receives the minimum facts needed to enforce the boundary. It does not receive ambient access to Hope memory, Tempus symbolism, Vitae history, or private journals.

## Strongest law — Protect the Container, Not Interpret the Person

> **Protection may secure the boundary around interior state; it may not convert interior meaning into a security classification of the person.**

Examples:

- encryption state may be checked;
- device trust may be checked;
- export scope may be checked;
- capability grants may be checked;
- storage integrity may be checked;
- a private reflection may not become a threat score merely because Protection can technically access the encrypted namespace.

## Candidate actions

Arcanum → Protection:

- request-private-export-check
- request-device-integrity-check
- request-recovery-path
- request-capability-review
- request-sensitive-share-confirmation

Protection → Arcanum:

- return-integrity-state
- deny-unauthorized-disclosure
- return-capability-diff
- return-recovery-options
- request-explicit-consent

## Faculty projection test

Hope, Tempus, and Vitae should be **default excluded** from Protection review.

If a narrow fact is needed, it should be derived rather than disclosed wholesale. Example: `contains_sensitive_private_context: true` is preferable to exposing the context itself.

## Candidate laws

1. **Interior Sovereignty Law** — private reflective meaning is not security telemetry.
2. **Boundary-Only Inspection** — Protection should inspect permissions, integrity, provenance, and disclosure scope before content.
3. **Recovery Without Identity Replacement** — device/key recovery must preserve continuity rather than manufacture a new person.
4. **No Protective Profiling** — psychological, ideological, spiritual, or developmental inference may not become a security score.
5. **Consent Escalation** — widening disclosure requires explicit authorization appropriate to the sensitivity.

## Orientation result

This edge is strong and clearly **operational**, not mirror-like. Protection serves Arcanum through privacy and capability assurance, but the two systems do not describe the same phenomenon from opposite poles.

That supports keeping **Arcanum ↔ Architect** as the deeper mirror axis while Arcanum ↔ Protection remains a cube-edge operational relationship.

## Open questions

- Can Protection enforce sensitivity classes without inspecting protected content?
- Which Arcanum exports require second-step confirmation?
- What recovery metadata can be shared without exposing interior history?
- How should compromised-device evidence affect local Hope continuity without deleting legitimate history?
