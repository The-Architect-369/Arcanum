# ARCnet Edge Study 24 — Theatrum ↔ Aerarium

## Candidate meaning
> **Culture ↔ Patronage**

Theatrum can create digital works, environments, public cultural resources, creator tools, and shared artistic infrastructure. Aerarium can support public cultural goods under legitimate Treasury/governance authority.

## Strongest law — Patronage Without Capture
> **Public funding may support cultural creation and infrastructure without buying creative meaning, ideological conformity, or prestige authority.**

## Candidate laws
1. **Funding–Meaning Separation** — receiving a grant does not transfer authorship or interpretive sovereignty to Aerarium.
2. **No Prestige Purchase** — public patronage must not create a system-wide rank of cultural worth.
3. **Transparent Grant Conditions** — amount, purpose, rights, deliverables, and duration are explicit before authorization.
4. **Rights Preservation** — grant support does not silently absorb creator ownership/licensing rights.
5. **Pluralism of Support** — funding rules should avoid turning one aesthetic or ideology into official culture absent explicit legitimate public mandate.
6. **Infrastructure Before Status** — shared rendering, storage, venues, archives, and accessibility may be stronger public-good candidates than popularity-driven awards.

## Orientation result
This is a strong operational edge and explains the earlier inner-junction concept of Creator Public Goods. It remains less mirror-like than Theatrum ↔ Protection: Aerarium supports cultural capacity, whereas Protection supplies the deeper counter-pole of integrity/provenance to expression/representation.
