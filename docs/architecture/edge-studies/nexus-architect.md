# ARCnet Edge Study 16 — Nexus ↔ Architect

## Candidate meaning

> **Community Signal ↔ Builder Response**

Nexus can surface discussion, requests, friction, proposals, and collaborative ideas. Architect can turn selected, reviewable community signals into design questions, issues, experiments, and implementation candidates.

## Core law — Popularity Is Not Product Authority

> **Community signal may inform what builders investigate; engagement volume does not automatically authorize what the system becomes.**

## Candidate flow

```text
Nexus discussion / request
      |
      | selected bounded synthesis
      v
Builder Signal Artifact
      |
      v
Architect issue / exploration / design candidate
      |
      | separate review
      v
possible implementation
```

## Candidate laws

1. **Signal–Mandate Separation** — discussion is evidence, not a binding product mandate.
2. **Attribution Without Mob Pressure** — provenance should preserve origin while avoiding popularity-as-authority.
3. **Minority Signal Preservation** — low-volume but high-consequence issues must not disappear under engagement ranking.
4. **Builder Independence With Accountability** — Architect may reject a suggestion with reasons; it may not falsely claim community endorsement.
5. **No Social Write Authority** — Nexus participation never grants repository or release permission.

## Orientation result

Strong operational relationship: community ↔ builder. It is not as deep a mirror as Nexus ↔ Imperium, because Architect turns signals into system design while Imperium turns collective input into formal decisions.
