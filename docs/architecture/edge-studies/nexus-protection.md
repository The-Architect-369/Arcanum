# ARCnet Edge Study 17 — Nexus ↔ Protection

## Candidate meaning

> **Social Freedom ↔ Social Safety**

Nexus enables relationship, discourse, publication, groups, events, and community. Protection supports privacy, abuse resistance, provenance, capability safety, and integrity controls.

## Strongest law — Safety Without Doctrine Sovereignty

> **Protection may enforce harm-, abuse-, privacy-, and integrity-based boundaries around Nexus without becoming the authority that decides acceptable belief or social meaning.**

## Candidate laws

1. **Moderation–Doctrine Separation** — safety controls do not define truth or ideology.
2. **Private Transport Integrity** — UI privacy claims must match actual delivery/storage boundaries.
3. **Abuse Evidence Minimization** — investigate harmful conduct without building broad social dossiers.
4. **Appealable Enforcement** — restrictions should be logged, scoped, reviewable, and proportionate.
5. **No Popularity Security Score** — social approval/disapproval is not a security classification.

## Orientation result

This is a necessary operational edge. It is not mirror-like because Protection bounds the conditions under which Nexus can remain free and safe; it does not represent Nexus's deep opposite. Nexus ↔ Imperium remains the stronger conversation ↔ decision mirror.
