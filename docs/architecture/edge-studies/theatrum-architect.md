# ARCnet Edge Study 22 — Theatrum ↔ Architect

## Candidate meaning
> **Creativity ↔ Creator Tools**

Theatrum expresses digital/symbolic artifacts, personas, environments, and culture. Architect supplies bounded creator tooling, rendering capabilities, package formats, extensions, and implementation pathways.

## Strongest law — Tooling Expands Expression, Not Authority
> **Creator tools may increase expressive capability without granting governance, security, or developmental authority.**

## Candidate laws
1. **Tool–Meaning Separation** — Architect can provide primitives without deciding what an artwork means.
2. **Extension–Authority Separation** — plugins/assets do not inherit repository or protocol authority.
3. **Creative Provenance** — assistant-assisted, collaborative, imported, and derivative work should remain distinguishable.
4. **No Prestige-by-Tooling** — access to advanced tools cannot imply human rank.
5. **Capability Disclosure** — creator packages declare what they can access or execute.

## Orientation result
Strong operational edge. It reinforces Theatrum ↔ Protection as the deeper mirror because Architect enables creation while Protection interrogates the integrity and provenance of what is represented.
