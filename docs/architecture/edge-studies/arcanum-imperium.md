# ARCnet Edge Study 14 — Arcanum ↔ Imperium

## Candidate meaning

> **Personal Experience ↔ Civic Participation**

Arcanum may help a person understand and articulate lived experience. Imperium may accept bounded civic input, proposals, review material, or votes where governance authority is otherwise valid.

The edge must prevent private becoming from becoming compulsory political evidence.

## Core flow

```text
Arcanum reflection / concern / proposal draft
        |
        | participant selects bounded derivative
        v
Civic Input Artifact
        |
        v
Imperium discussion / proposal / review path
        |
        | separate governance rules
        v
authorization outcome
```

## Strongest law — Experience May Inform Governance; It Does Not Become Governance

> **Lived experience may be expressed as civic evidence or testimony, but personal meaning does not acquire binding authority merely by entering Imperium.**

A Hope reflection is not a vote. A Vitae recognition is not automatic governing power. A Tempus symbolic interpretation is not a public policy mandate.

## Candidate laws

1. **Voluntary Civic Projection** — private Arcanum state enters Imperium only through explicit participant selection.
2. **Testimony–Authority Separation** — testimony can inform a decision without becoming the decision.
3. **No Interior Qualification Test** — governance participation must not require exposing private Hope or symbolic history merely to prove civic legitimacy.
4. **Eligibility Is Bounded** — where Vitae can contribute to eligibility under canon, the projection should expose only the relevant responsibility envelope, not private developmental history.
5. **Metaphysical Neutrality in Public Authority** — symbolic Tempus content may be discussed but may not become binding public authority merely through interpretation.

## Orientation result

This relationship is clearly useful, but **Arcanum ↔ Imperium** reads as a passage from person to civic system, not as a deep antipodal duality. The current Arcanum ↔ Architect mirror remains stronger.
