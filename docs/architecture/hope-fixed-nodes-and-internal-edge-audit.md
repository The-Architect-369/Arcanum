# Hope Fixed Relational Nodes and Internal Edge Audit

## Purpose

This document records the current working architecture decision for **Hope A2 / Inner Portrait** and runs the first complete edge audit of that inner geometry.

The decision under test is:

> **Hope may use a fixed six-direction relational scaffold around sovereign Identity, while the content observed at those coordinates remains participant-owned, revisable, private by default, and non-diagnostic. Planetary archetypes rotate across the relationships as optional lenses; they do not own the nodes.**

This is a design-candidate decision on the Creation Era architecture branch. It does not amend canonical module architecture.

## Center first

Canonical Identity remains controlling:

```text
Identity = continuity
not description
not personality
not rank
not reputation
```

Therefore the center is:

```text
SELF / IDENTITY
sovereign continuity
```

The prior `Presence` facet is better treated as a **center-state / orientation** than as one of six lateral directions.

A useful distinction is:

```text
Identity
= the sovereign center itself

Presence
= how available / illuminated / attended the center currently is

Sun
= optional symbol of illumination, coherence, and return-to-center
```

The Sun is not Identity.

The Sun is not a score.

The Sun does not literally grow as an objective measure of human worth.

A future interface may visualize increasing **relational illumination / articulated context** as more relationships are consciously explored, but that visualization must mean only:

```text
more participant-owned relational context is currently available
```

and never:

```text
better person
more evolved person
higher human value
more governance authority
more Vitae worth
```

Candidate law:

> **Illumination Measures Articulation, Not Worth — a brighter or more complete center visualization may represent currently articulated relationships, never personal superiority, virtue, maturity, or authority.**

# Six fixed structural directions

The strongest current six-node set is:

```text
RECEPTIVITY
what am I receiving, sensing, remembering, or noticing change within?

EXPRESSION
what is seeking form, language, representation, or creation?

RELATION
what is being met, joined, valued, reciprocated, or held with another?

AGENCY
what can I choose, initiate, refuse, assert, or enact?

DISCERNMENT
what am I differentiating, understanding, judging, or synthesizing?

RESPONSIBILITY
what boundary, commitment, consequence, stewardship, or durable structure is mine to carry?
```

These are **relational functions**, not virtues or personality traits.

A participant can leave a node blank, reject Hope's language, or use a personal label in the UI while the underlying structural coordinate remains stable.

Candidate law:

> **Fixed Coordinate, Participant Meaning — Hope may stabilize the relational coordinate while leaving the participant sovereign over the meaning, wording, observation, and relevance occupying that coordinate.**

## Why these six are stronger than the earlier prototype

Earlier A2 exploration used:

```text
Presence
Agency
Relation
Care
Discernment
Expression
```

The current refinement does three things:

1. moves **Presence** to the center, where it functions more naturally as present-centered orientation;
2. expands **Care** into **Responsibility**, because care, duty, limit, stewardship, and consequence are not identical but share a durable relational boundary;
3. adds **Receptivity** explicitly so the architecture has a non-executive receiving direction rather than forcing every reflection into agency, expression, or judgment.

The result also produces three unusually coherent deep axes.

# Three center-mediated axes

```text
RECEPTIVITY ↔ RESPONSIBILITY
what is arriving / changing
↔
what must be bounded / carried / made durable

EXPRESSION ↔ DISCERNMENT
what is being represented
↔
what is being understood / differentiated / judged

RELATION ↔ AGENCY
mutual encounter / affinity / reciprocity
↔
self-direction / assertion / action
```

These are not octahedral edges. They pass through the sovereign center.

They should never be optimized toward equal balance.

## Axis 1 — Receptivity ↔ Responsibility

Core question:

> What am I receiving or experiencing, and what do I consciously choose to carry, bound, preserve, or release?

Failure modes:

- treating emotion as irresponsibility;
- treating duty as suppression;
- converting temporary feeling into permanent obligation;
- converting obligation into total identity.

Finding: **strong**.

## Axis 2 — Expression ↔ Discernment

Core question:

> Does what I am expressing faithfully represent what I currently understand, and does my interpretation survive contact with how I am representing it?

Failure modes:

- eloquence mistaken for truth;
- analysis becoming self-silencing;
- interpretation hardened into diagnosis;
- expression treated as proof of inner essence.

Finding: **strong**.

## Axis 3 — Relation ↔ Agency

Core question:

> How can connection, reciprocity, and care coexist with voluntary choice, boundary, refusal, and action?

Failure modes:

- affinity mistaken for consent;
- agency treated as isolation;
- relationship treated as obligation;
- conflict treated as relational failure.

Finding: **very strong**.

# Twelve neighboring Hope edges

A regular octahedron has twelve edges. With the three antipodal pairs above, each node connects directly to the four nodes that are not itself or its opposite.

The twelve relations are:

```text
01 Receptivity ↔ Expression
02 Receptivity ↔ Relation
03 Receptivity ↔ Agency
04 Receptivity ↔ Discernment

05 Responsibility ↔ Expression
06 Responsibility ↔ Relation
07 Responsibility ↔ Agency
08 Responsibility ↔ Discernment

09 Expression ↔ Relation
10 Expression ↔ Agency
11 Discernment ↔ Relation
12 Discernment ↔ Agency
```

The audit question is:

> Does each relationship expose a distinct and useful reflective function without inventing a trait, score, diagnosis, or hidden authority?

---

## 01 — Receptivity ↔ Expression

Function:

```text
receive / notice / remember
↔
form / speak / create
```

Useful questions:

- What are you receiving before you respond?
- What changed between the experience and its expression?
- What is lost or added when the inner experience becomes language or form?

Edge finding:

> **Reception–Representation Boundary — what is experienced and what is expressed are related but not identical records.**

Why distinct:

This prevents Hope from assuming that a statement perfectly exposes an interior state.

Result: **strong**.

---

## 02 — Receptivity ↔ Relation

Function:

```text
what arrives / is sensed
↔
how another is encountered
```

Useful questions:

- What are you actually receiving from the other person versus anticipating?
- What response belongs to the present interaction and what belongs to memory?
- Is openness being confused with agreement?

Edge finding:

> **Reception Does Not Equal Consent — perceiving, hearing, empathizing, or receiving another does not itself create agreement, obligation, or permission.**

Result: **strong**.

---

## 03 — Receptivity ↔ Agency

Function:

```text
notice / receive
↔
choose / act
```

Useful questions:

- What is present before action?
- Which part of the impulse is observation and which part is already a choice?
- Is action actually required now?

Edge finding:

> **Perception–Action Separation — a felt or observed state does not automatically authorize or require action.**

This strongly matches Hope's non-commanding doctrine.

Result: **strong**.

---

## 04 — Receptivity ↔ Discernment

Function:

```text
receive / remember / notice change
↔
interpret / distinguish / synthesize
```

Useful questions:

- What was observed before interpretation?
- What might be memory, inference, assumption, or current evidence?
- Has the situation changed enough that the old interpretation should expire?

Edge finding:

> **Observation–Interpretation Separation — Hope must preserve the provenance boundary between received experience and derived meaning.**

This directly supports Reflective State Expiry.

Result: **very strong**.

---

## 05 — Responsibility ↔ Expression

Function:

```text
commitment / boundary / consequence
↔
representation / publication / creation
```

Useful questions:

- What responsibility follows from saying or publishing this?
- What should remain private or unfinished?
- What authorship, consent, attribution, or commitment boundary applies?

Edge finding:

> **Expression Has Consequence Without Becoming Identity — publication may create responsibility, but the artifact does not become an exhaustive definition of the person.**

Result: **strong**.

---

## 06 — Responsibility ↔ Relation

Function:

```text
commitment / boundary
↔
reciprocity / connection
```

Useful questions:

- What has actually been promised?
- What responsibility is yours versus merely expected by another?
- What boundary protects voluntary relation from compulsory care?

Edge finding:

> **Relation Does Not Manufacture Duty — connection, affinity, kinship, or closeness does not silently create unlimited obligation.**

Result: **very strong**.

---

## 07 — Responsibility ↔ Agency

Function:

```text
constraint / stewardship / consequence
↔
choice / assertion / action
```

Useful questions:

- What choices remain inside the real responsibility envelope?
- Which consequences are foreseeable before acting?
- What duty limits action, and what action is necessary to fulfill duty?

Edge finding:

> **Agency Operates Inside Explicit Consequence — sovereignty includes the ability to choose while making relevant responsibility visible before consequential action.**

This is strongly analogous to ARCnet's pre-authorization consequence rules without making the inner model executable.

Result: **very strong**.

---

## 08 — Responsibility ↔ Discernment

Function:

```text
what must be carried / bounded
↔
what is understood / judged
```

Useful questions:

- What evidence is sufficient before adopting a durable conclusion?
- Is a temporary interpretation becoming a permanent responsibility?
- What responsibility follows if the judgment is wrong?

Edge finding:

> **Durability Requires Stronger Epistemic Ground — the more persistent the commitment or conclusion, the more explicitly Hope should distinguish evidence, inference, and uncertainty.**

Result: **very strong**.

---

## 09 — Expression ↔ Relation

Function:

```text
representation / communication
↔
encounter / reciprocity / audience
```

Useful questions:

- What was intended, communicated, and received?
- What expression belongs to you and what meaning belongs to the receiver?
- What consent or audience boundary applies?

Edge finding:

> **Expression Does Not Control Reception — authorship grants responsibility for the expression, not sovereignty over another person's interpretation.**

Result: **strong**.

---

## 10 — Expression ↔ Agency

Function:

```text
say / symbolize / create
↔
act / assert / refuse
```

Useful questions:

- Is this a thought, draft, statement, request, promise, or action?
- Does speaking this create a commitment?
- What would actually cross from expression into execution?

Edge finding:

> **Expression–Execution Separation — reflection, language, symbolism, drafting, and intention are not automatically actions.**

This is one of the strongest cross-scale parallels with ARCnet's Two-Phase Passage.

Result: **very strong**.

---

## 11 — Discernment ↔ Relation

Function:

```text
interpret / distinguish / judge
↔
encounter / affinity / reciprocity
```

Useful questions:

- What do you know about the relationship versus infer about the other person?
- Is disagreement being converted into a judgment of worth?
- What interpretation preserves pluralism and interior sovereignty?

Edge finding:

> **Relational Judgment Must Not Become Human-Worth Judgment — discernment may evaluate situations, agreements, behavior, or fit without assigning global value to a person.**

Result: **very strong**.

---

## 12 — Discernment ↔ Agency

Function:

```text
understand / judge / synthesize
↔
choose / act
```

Useful questions:

- What judgment is actually necessary before acting?
- What remains uncertain?
- Is the action reversible if the interpretation is incomplete?

Edge finding:

> **Judgment Does Not Self-Ratify Action — interpretation can inform a choice but cannot manufacture authority or certainty.**

Result: **very strong**.

# Internal edge audit result

All twelve neighboring relationships produced distinct reflective questions without requiring a seventh lateral category or a personality taxonomy.

The six-node set therefore **survives the first complete internal-edge audit** as a coherent A2 scaffold.

The strongest recurring internal laws are:

1. **Observation–Interpretation Separation**;
2. **Perception–Action Separation**;
3. **Expression–Execution Separation**;
4. **Reception Does Not Equal Consent**;
5. **Relation Does Not Manufacture Duty**;
6. **Relational Judgment Must Not Become Human-Worth Judgment**;
7. **Durability Requires Stronger Epistemic Ground**;
8. **Fixed Coordinate, Participant Meaning**;
9. **Illumination Measures Articulation, Not Worth**.

These should be treated as Hope A2 design constraints, not universal psychological laws.

# Planetary lens relationship

The six nodes are **not** permanently assigned to planets.

The earlier lens audit remains controlling:

```text
planetary resonance = optional metadata
planetary lens = explicit interpretive operation
planetary identity = rejected
planetary authority = prohibited
```

A node or edge can therefore be examined through:

```text
Moon
Mercury
Venus
Mars
Jupiter
Saturn
```

without changing its structural identity.

Example:

```text
Relation ↔ Agency

Moon lens
What are you receiving or remembering before responding?

Mercury lens
What has actually been communicated?

Venus lens
What is valued in the relationship?

Mars lens
What boundary or action is required?

Jupiter lens
What larger judgment or meaning are you attaching?

Saturn lens
What responsibility or durable limit applies?
```

This produces:

```text
12 structural relations
×
6 optional lens families
=
72 possible relation-lens interrogations
```

The number is a combinatorial UI fact, not sacred proof and not a requirement that every participant use all 72.

Candidate law:

> **No Completion Through Enumeration — combinatorial coverage describes available questions, not a required reflective journey or achievement state.**

# Hope, Tempus, and Vitae relationship

The fixed A2 scaffold gives the three identity-centered faculties clearer separation.

```text
HOPE
What is in relationship now?

TEMPUS
How has the selected relation changed through time, and what temporal context is relevant?

VITAE
What practice or responsibility has actually stabilized enough to be recognized separately?
```

Hope does not become Tempus by storing time.

Hope does not become Vitae by naming growth.

Instead:

```text
A2 relation
   ↓ explicit participant selection
Tempus context projection
   ↓ optional comparison through time
participant interpretation
   ↓ separate deliberate pathway if appropriate
Vitae recognition process
```

No automatic A2 → Vitae promotion is permitted.

# C2 Natal Pattern boundary

C2 remains a slower symbolic/foundational layer.

Its candidate role is:

```text
chosen symbolic coordinate systems
historical/cultural interpretation packs
optional natal/astronomical context
participant-authored meaning
```

A2 remains:

```text
current relational snapshot
```

Therefore:

> **C2 may offer lenses to A2; it may not pre-fill, score, predict, or dictate A2 nodes.**

Tempus is the natural source of astronomical facts and time-indexed celestial state.

Hope may receive only bounded, explicitly selected symbolic context.

# Economic incentive boundary

A separate idea under discussion is using MANA to encourage early Hope engagement or reflection.

This document does **not** adopt that mechanism.

Current economic doctrine requires:

```text
Payment may expand capacity.
It may not accelerate becoming.
```

MANA also has unresolved issuance/emission authority.

Directly rewarding reflection volume, node completion, lens use, streaks, or perceived inner development would create serious risks:

- gaming reflection;
- turning Hope into productivity behavior;
- creating implicit worth metrics;
- coercive engagement;
- linking economic value to becoming;
- violating Hope anti-streak / anti-dependency constraints.

Therefore candidate law:

> **Reflection Is Not Mineable — Hope reflection, A2 completion, archetypal use, or declared inner state must not directly mint or determine MANA rewards.**

If economic onboarding incentives are later desired, they require a separate Economic Constitution / Hope doctrine audit and should reward bounded protocol participation or capacity-building actions rather than claimed interior states.
