# Creation Era Architecture Trail

## Purpose

This document preserves a bounded post-Wave-XXIV architecture exploration so later Architect sessions do not have to reconstruct the reasoning from chat history.

It is **design evidence, not ratified architecture**. Existing canonical doctrine, economic authority, governance, Treasury rules, identity rules, and native decisions remain controlling until explicitly amended.

## Era naming boundary

Wave XXIV closure used **ARCnet Construction Era** for the post-canonicalization development period. The subsequent exploration used **Creation Era** as a working name.

These names must not be silently treated as interchangeable canon. A later review must decide whether:

- Creation Era replaces Construction Era;
- Construction Era remains the formal era while Creation Era names an architectural/design phase within it; or
- another hierarchy better preserves both meanings.

Until then, this document uses **Creation Era** as working language only.

## Eight-point topology hypothesis

The leading macro-system model is an eight-point star-tetrahedral / cube correspondence used as a semantic system graph rather than mandatory user navigation.

### Generative / lived tetrahedron

1. **Arcanum** — personal development, reflection, practice, becoming.
2. **Nexus** — social relation, discourse, publication, community.
3. **Commercium** — physical craft, services, exchange.
4. **Theatrum** — digital creation, aesthetics, symbolic and digital artifacts.

### Stewardship / system tetrahedron

1. **Architect** — system design and development.
2. **Protection** — security, provenance, integrity, capability safety.
3. **Imperium** — formal governance and civic coordination.
4. **Aerarium** — shared wealth and the public-facing treasury/economic domain.

`Aerarium` is not yet a settled name. Earlier exploration also produced `Aeatrium` and `Ararium`. Naming should be decided separately from topology.

## Center, field, and flow

### Human / Identity — center

Identity is not a ninth app. The human remains the sovereign actor at the center of the topology.

The geometry must remain subordinate to the canonical Identity Model: identity is continuity, not description, rank, wallet balance, or prediction.

### ARCnet — field

ARCnet is the enclosing execution, capability, transport, receipt, and settlement field. It is not another peer system point.

### MANA — flow

MANA is better modeled as a value/capacity flow through legitimate contracts rather than as a geometric system point owned by one application.

## Four mirror axes

The proposed deep oppositions are:

- **Arcanum ↔ Architect** — human development ↔ system development.
- **Nexus ↔ Imperium** — social emergence ↔ formal collective decision.
- **Commercium ↔ Aerarium** — private/distributed exchange ↔ common-resource stewardship.
- **Theatrum ↔ Protection** — expression/creation ↔ integrity/provenance.

These oppositions describe balancing relationships, not authority inheritance.

## Twenty-eight relationship hypothesis

Eight points produce 28 unique pairwise relationships.

The geometry suggests three semantic classes:

- **12 kinship relationships** — six edges within each tetrahedron;
- **12 operational relationships** — cube-edge cross-tetrahedron relations;
- **4 mirror relationships** — deep opposite/body-diagonal pairs.

Each system therefore has seven relationships: three kinship, three operational, and one mirror.

### Critical rule

**Geometry does not authorize behavior.**

A geometric relation only asks whether a meaningful correspondence exists. Each candidate edge must independently justify:

- legitimate directional actions;
- data/disclosure boundaries;
- required capabilities;
- economic effects;
- settlement needs;
- doctrine guards;
- failure and revocation behavior.

If an edge cannot justify a legitimate function, symmetry does not require the system to invent one.

## Candidate Arcanum restructuring

The exploration suggests a future Arcanum centered on three internal human-development faculties:

- **Hope** — reflection and interior interpretation;
- **Tempus** — time, rhythm, correspondence, ritual context;
- **Vitae** — practice, learning, retrospective recognition, and becoming.

This would move **Nexus** to its own major ARCnet system and stop assuming that Wallet is a peer Arcanum module.

This is a major departure from the current canonical flat module registry and therefore requires a future Module Architecture v2 review before implementation.

Leading correspondences to test are:

- Arcanum → Nexus through Hope: **reflection → relation/expression**;
- Arcanum → Commercium through Tempus: **correspondence/timing → physical craft**;
- Arcanum → Theatrum through Vitae: **journey/practice → symbolic/digital expression**.

These are design candidates, not ratified boundaries.

## Sacred geometry and user-interface separation

The exploration rejected the idea that geometric topology must become literal primary navigation.

Candidate separation:

- **Sacred geometry** — ontology, correspondence, knowledge structure, and relationship graph.
- **ARCnet Edge Contracts** — executable cross-system boundaries.
- **Cards, pages, tabs, footers, and the ARCnet system dial** — human-facing interaction language.

The card/deck metaphor remains the strongest general Arcanum UI concept. Geometry may become directly visible inside modules such as Hope, Tempus, or Vitae when the geometry itself helps express their meaning, without forcing commerce, governance, or social interaction into the same visual mechanism.

## Ideas no longer leading the architecture

The following remain useful historical exploration but are not the leading macro-system model:

- dodecahedron as mandatory primary navigation;
- forcing each visual tab to equal a geometric edge;
- inventing modules or systems solely to fill geometric capacity;
- making sacred geometry literal chrome across every surface;
- assigning MANA to one system point;
- assuming all 28 edges are valid merely because the graph permits them.

The dodecahedron/icosahedron work may remain valuable for Tempus/Vitae knowledge geometry and should not be discarded as historical design evidence.
