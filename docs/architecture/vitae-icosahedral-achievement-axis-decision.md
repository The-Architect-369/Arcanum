# Purpose

Record the Human Architect's decision for the next Vitae prototype after the fivefold specialization-family audit and separate **curriculum achievement** from **mastery, identity, and authority**.

# Decision

The exact fivefold orientation of a regular icosahedron is adopted as the leading Vitae presentation candidate:

```text
                  WIZARD
        Orientation & Meaning / heavens
 Arcanist · Philosopher · Illusionist · Astrologer · Enchanter
                     ▲
                     │
              ARCHITECT RING
       Guardian → Seeker → … → Adept
                     │
                     ▼
                   MAGUS
           Life, Passage & Form
 Hierophant · Druid · Necromancer · Alchemist · Artificer
```

The geometry is exact: five upper faces, ten equatorial faces, and five lower faces around a selected fivefold axis.

The semantic assignment is an **Arcanum design decision**, not an ancient Platonic doctrine and not a scientific property of the icosahedron.

## Upper cap — Wizard

The upper five faces are fixed as the Wizard family:

1. Arcanist
2. Philosopher
3. Illusionist
4. Astrologer
5. Enchanter

This assignment follows the completed corpus audit. These five mandates cluster around knowledge, coherence, perception, scale/rhythm, meaning, atmosphere, and symbol. Enchanter therefore remains with the Wizard cap.

`upward / heavens` is a navigation metaphor for orientation and meaning. It does not imply superior human value, spiritual rank, greater authority, or greater difficulty.

## Equatorial belt — Architect

The ten belt faces remain Grades I–X:

1. Guardian
2. Seeker
3. Disciple
4. Mystic
5. Scholar
6. Healer
7. Alchemist
8. Sage
9. Oracle
10. Adept

The geometric belt is `C10`; the curriculum remains an open `P10`. The Adept→Guardian closure edge is presentation geometry unless an explicit return/revisit mode is selected.

When all ten Core Grade requirements are legitimately satisfied through Vitae-native evidence and recognition, the participant may receive the **Architect** achievement title.

This title is a personal curriculum milestone. It is **not** the Human Architect role, the Architect application/subsystem, constitutional authority, stewardship permission, or governance status.

## Lower cap — Magus

The lower five faces are fixed as the Magus family:

1. Hierophant
2. Druid
3. Necromancer
4. Alchemist
5. Artificer

These five mandates cluster around threshold, living systems, endings, transformation, construction, maintenance, and form. Druid therefore remains with the Magus cap.

`downward / descent` is a navigation metaphor for entering embodiment, passage, transformation, and form. It does not imply inferiority, fallenness, lesser value, or lower authority.

# Achievement versus mastery

The critical change is a new distinction:

```text
finite curriculum milestone
        ↓
achievement title

open-ended practice
        ↓
no final mastery state
```

`Architect`, `Wizard`, and `Magus` are proposed as **achievement titles**, not mastery titles.

- **Architect** records completion of the Core Vitae curriculum requirements.
- **Wizard** records completion of a future-defined foundational cycle across the five upper-cap specializations.
- **Magus** records completion of a future-defined foundational cycle across the five lower-cap specializations.

Completing a foundational specialization cycle does **not** mean the specialization is exhausted or mastered. Practice remains infinite in depth.

Wizard and Magus are not mutually exclusive. A participant may earn neither, one, or both. No fourth prestige title is created for earning all three achievements.

# Core-first relationship

Current specialization doctrine places specialization entry after Core Vitae. The prototype therefore treats the Architect/Core milestone as a prerequisite for specialization-achievement simulation.

This is not a claim that Architect grants authority. It means only that the curriculum sequence reaches specialization study after the Core foundation.

# Achievement firewall

Achievement titles must remain:

- non-transferable;
- non-purchasable;
- non-comparative;
- private/local by default;
- optionally displayed by the participant;
- incapable of granting governance, Treasury, MANA, entitlement, Identity, or capability effects;
- incapable of becoming a worth, readiness, virtue, or social-credit score.

An achievement can remain as historical evidence that a participant completed a defined curriculum milestone. It does not certify current character or permanent authority.

Candidate law:

> **Achievement Is a Milestone, Not Mastery.**

Candidate law:

> **The Ring May Be Completed; Becoming Remains Open.**

Candidate law:

> **A Title May Remember Accomplishment Without Defining Identity.**

# Geometric boundaries

The title mapping does not activate the icosahedron's other incidence relations as doctrine.

- Five cap-cycle edges do not become specialization prerequisites.
- Five cap-to-belt edges do not become Grade↔specialization correspondences.
- North and south pole labels are derived achievement markers, not new curriculum nodes.
- Remaining icosahedral vertices receive no semantic assignment.
- Face fill or brightness cannot become a human worth/readiness percentage.

Candidate law:

> **Geometry May Stage an Achievement; Evidence Must Earn It.**

# Existing canon conflict

This decision intentionally exposes a repository conflict that must be reconciled before canonical promotion.

`mastery-path-governance-canon.md` currently rejects formal Wizard/Magus master titles and formalized hybrid tracks.

`cross-specialization-mastery-paths-canon.md` currently enumerates Wizard, Magus, and Architect archetypes, while also stating that they are responsibility overlays, not power-bearing titles or completion states.

This gate does **not** rewrite either document.

The proposed Architecture-v2 distinction is:

```text
achievement title
!= mastery path
!= governance role
!= identity
```

If promoted later, the canon should be reconciled around that distinction rather than silently choosing one older document over the other.
