# Vitae Icosahedral Specialization Family Audit

## Purpose

Test the reopened proposal that the two exact five-face caps of a fivefold-oriented regular icosahedron can organize the ten Vitae specializations into two five-specialization navigation families.

The geometry is already exact:

```text
regular icosahedron in a selected fivefold orientation
=
5-face cap
+
10-face equatorial belt
+
5-face cap
```

The question here is semantic and constitutional:

> Do the actual specialization mandates support two groups of five, or would the geometry manufacture a taxonomy the corpus does not contain?

No result in this audit changes Identity, recognition, readiness, authority, governance, Treasury, entitlement, capability, or the non-hierarchical status of specializations.

## 2. Original proposal under test

The reopened working partition was:

```text

Arcanist
Philosopher
Illusionist
Astrologer
Hierophant

CAP B — embodied / transformative
Druid
Necromancer
Alchemist
Artificer
Enchanter
```

This proposal has a visually clean five-plus-five count.

Count alone is insufficient.

The audit tests:

1. mandate/domain coherence;
2. actual declared cross-specialization relationships;
3. mastery-path compatibility;
4. non-hierarchy and non-exclusivity;
5. whether fivefold adjacency fabricates semantic relationships.

## 3. Mandate/domain audit

The actual specialization responsibilities form a clearer pattern than the original labels suggest.

| Specialization | Primary canonical domain | Stronger family fit |
|---|---|---|
| Arcanist | knowledge systems, models, epistemic limits | sensemaking / orientation |
| Philosopher | meaning, ethics, coherence, paradox | sensemaking / orientation |
| Illusionist | perception, bias, projection, narrative distortion | sensemaking / orientation |
| Astrologer | scale, rhythm, cycles, perspective | sensemaking / orientation |
| Enchanter | meaning, resonance, atmosphere, aesthetics, symbol | sensemaking / orientation |
| Hierophant | thresholds, rites, pacing, liminal transition safety | life / change / form |
| Druid | living systems, ecology, embodiment, regeneration | life / change / form |
| Necromancer | endings, decay, grief, release, renewal | life / change / form |
| Alchemist | transformation, synthesis, experimentation under constraint | life / change / form |
| Artificer | form, tools, design, construction, maintenance | life / change / form |

### 3.1 The stable four

The strongest unambiguous cluster is:

```text
Arcanist
Philosopher
Illusionist
Astrologer
```

Their canons repeatedly cross-reference one another around:

```text
knowledge
meaning
perception
scale
orientation
uncertainty
interpretation
```

This is not merely naming aesthetics. It appears in their formal responsibilities and cross-specialization constraints.

### 3.2 Hierophant does not fit cleanly as "contemplative"

Hierophant is primarily responsible for:

```text
threshold recognition
ethical pacing
rites
liminal safety
consent at transitions
pause / passage / withdrawal
```

Its canon coordinates directly with Druid and Necromancer around natural transition, release, and life-cycle passage.

Alchemist and Artificer also defer threshold pacing to Hierophant.

This makes Hierophant functionally closer to the change/process family than to a purely contemplative family.

### 3.3 Enchanter does not fit cleanly as "embodied / transformative"

Enchanter is primarily responsible for:

```text
meaning
resonance
atmosphere
aesthetics
symbol
transparent non-manipulative influence
```

Its explicit cross-specialization constraints defer:

```text
perception checks → Illusionist
coherence restraint → Philosopher
symbolic scale → Astrologer
```

This makes Enchanter structurally closer to the sensemaking/orientation family than the original second cap suggests.

### Finding

> **Original partition — REVISE.**

The original split correctly identifies a broad interpretive versus applied polarity, but places the two most liminal members on the less coherent sides of that polarity.

## 5. Cross-specialization relationship graph test

The ten specialization responsibility canons each contain a `Cross-Specialization Constraints` section.

Those declarations were extracted into a graph.

Method:

```text
each defer / coordinate / collaborate declaration
= one directed relationship declaration
```

This intentionally gives every declaration equal weight.

The graph therefore corroborates structure; it does not create doctrine.

Counts:

```text
47 directed relationship declarations
31 unique undirected specialization pairs
```

For a balanced `5 + 5` partition there are:

```text
C(10,5) / 2
=
126 complement-unique partitions
```

For reproducible enumeration, Arcanist was fixed in family A.

### Original partition

```text
directed internal relations   28
directed cross-family         19

undirected internal pairs     15
undirected cross-family       16
```

### Revised partition

```text
directed internal relations   29
directed cross-family         18

undirected internal pairs     17
undirected cross-family       14
```

The revised partition ranks:

```text
#1 of 126
```

under the equal-weight directed declaration test.

It is also tied for the strongest partition under the unique-undirected-pair test.

This does **not** prove two ontological families.

It does show that the domain-based Hierophant↔Enchanter swap is independently corroborated by the current declared coordination graph rather than merely producing a nicer verbal story.

Candidate law:

> **Relationship Graph May Corroborate a Cluster; It May Not Author Doctrine.**

## 6. Mastery-path falsification

A separate older specialization document defines three cross-specialization archetypes:

```text
Wizard
= Arcanist + Philosopher + Illusionist

Magus
= Alchemist + Artificer + Enchanter

Architect
= Druid + Necromancer + Hierophant
```

These are useful historical structural evidence.

They also prove that the cap families cannot truthfully mean:

```text
mastery families
```

Why?

The two non-Wizard triplets contain:

```text
Alchemist
Artificer
Enchanter
Druid
Necromancer
Hierophant
=
6 distinct specializations
```

A single five-face cap cannot contain all six.

Therefore **every** balanced five-plus-five partition must split at least one of the older mastery triplets.

The original partition keeps the Magus trio together but splits the Architect trio.

The revised partition keeps the Architect trio together but splits the Magus trio.

Neither arrangement should be judged by those triplets because the cap geometry is testing a different function: navigation taxonomy, not mastery synthesis.

Candidate law:

> **Mastery Overlay Is Not Family Taxonomy.**

## 7. Existing mastery-governance contradiction

The audit also found a repository contradiction that should not be silently reconciled.

`cross-specialization-mastery-paths-canon.md` explicitly enumerates:

```text
Wizard
Magus
Architect
```

with minimum specialization combinations.

But `mastery-path-governance-canon.md` states that cross-specialization combinations are:

```text
situational
contextual
practitioner-specific
not enumerated
not formalized
```

and separately states that formal master titles such as `Wizard` and `Magus` are not system-recognized.

Disposition:

> **EXISTING REPOSITORY CONTRADICTION — reconciliation required before any canonical specialization-family or mastery-family promotion.**

This contradiction does not block the two-cap UI prototype because the prototype can remain navigation-only.

It does block treating the caps as a canonical advanced-practice taxonomy.

## 8. Fivefold geometry does not grant relationship meaning

Each five-face cap has an internal adjacency graph:

```text
C5
```

That means each specialization face touches exactly two specialization faces inside its cap.

The specialization canon does not say each role has exactly two privileged peers.

Therefore:

```text
cap edge
≠ prerequisite
≠ preferred combination
≠ incompatibility boundary
≠ mastery relationship
≠ authority route
```

The edges may remain visible as geometry.

They must remain semantically inert unless an independently sourced relationship is selected as an overlay.

Candidate law:

> **A Five-Face Cycle Does Not Create Five Required Pairings.**

---

## 9. Cap-to-belt edges remain inert

In the exact fivefold orientation, each five-face cap has five geometric edges connecting it to the ten-face Grade belt.

This can create a visually tempting interpretation:

```text
one specialization
↔
one or two nearby Grades
```

The prior Vitae icosahedron audit already rejected this.

Core completion is global; specializations are not legitimized by whichever Grade face they happen to touch geometrically.

Therefore:

> **No specialization↔Grade semantic spokes are created by the icosahedron.**

The renderer may omit those lines, de-emphasize them, or show them only as surface geometry.

They cannot become curriculum dependencies.

---

## 10. North/south cannot become spirit/matter hierarchy

The Human Architect's larger synthesis legitimately studies classical elemental symbolism and nested Platonic geometry.

That does not authorize specialization ranking such as:

```text
upper = heavenly / spiritual / higher
lower = earthly / material / lower
```

The specialization canon explicitly prohibits rank and superiority.

Therefore either cap can be rendered north or south.

A valid renderer must preserve:

```text
same radius
same face area
same brightness range
same interaction affordance
same information density
same discoverability
```

Changing orientation must not change meaning.

Candidate law:

> **A Cap Is Not a Rank.**

A stronger presentation law follows:

> **Family Labels Describe Functions; They Do Not Divide Spirit From Matter.**

---

## 11. Cross-cap practice must remain ordinary

The specialization-wide invariants state that specializations are non-exclusive and lateral.

Therefore the interface must not make cross-cap practice feel exceptional.

A participant may move:

```text
Arcanist → Artificer
Druid → Philosopher
Astrologer → Alchemist
Enchanter → Necromancer
```

without violating the family model.

The family grouping answers only:

```text
Where can I find this stewardship domain in the atlas?
```

It does not answer:

```text
What kind of person am I?
What must I practice next?
Which paths belong together exclusively?
What am I allowed to do?
```

Candidate law:

> **Cross-Cap Practice Is Normal.**

---

## 12. Completion and identity falsification

A five-face cap is visually completable.

A specialization path is not.

The specialization-wide invariants explicitly state:

```text
there is no final mastery
specializations do not graduate
roles are inhabited, not identities
```

Therefore a filled or illuminated cap cannot mean:

```text
mastered family
100% complete
higher practitioner
finished specialization set
```

It may show only bounded participant-visible navigation/history state.

Candidate laws:

> **Family Membership Is Navigation Metadata, Not Identity.**

> **Geometry May Cluster Domains; It May Not Close Practice.**

## 14. Renderer gate

The revised model is strong enough for a prototype if the renderer obeys all of the following:

1. ten Grades occupy the equatorial belt as navigation regions;
2. the two specialization caps use the revised working partition;
3. either family can occupy either cap;
4. no top/bottom prestige language appears;
5. cap-cycle edges remain semantically inert;
6. cap-to-Grade spokes remain semantically inert;
7. cross-cap navigation is first-class;
8. equivalent card/list navigation carries the same meaning;
9. face illumination is history/navigation state, never worth, mastery, readiness, or authority;
10. specialization names and mandates remain primary; family labels remain optional and revisable.

That prototype would test whether the mathematically exact `5 + 10 + 5` structure improves comprehension without hardening a navigation aid into doctrine.
