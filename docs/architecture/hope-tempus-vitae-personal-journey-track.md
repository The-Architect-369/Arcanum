# Architect's Arcanum: HOPE–TEMPUS–VITAE Personal Journey and Architect Path

## Purpose

This track develops a lived, personal Arcanum in which HOPE learns through humane conversation, helps the participant hold a changing picture of life, offers a small number of useful next actions, and binds chosen action into TEMPUS rites without becoming a judge, scheduler, diagnostician, or sovereign actor.

It also examines how the Vitae curriculum can teach the capabilities required for a HOPE journey and how increasingly capable participants can gain more powerful personal authoring tools without turning Vitae into rank, worth, economic privilege, or automatic authority.

This is a reviewable design track. It does not amend doctrine, activate MANA pricing, grant Architect authority, or implement runtime behavior.

## Authority and decision posture

### Known controlling constraints

- Identity remains sovereign and is not reducible to a profile, score, balance, symbolic map, or predicted personality.
- HOPE may mirror, clarify, ask, and draft; it may not command, diagnose, ratify, confirm readiness, or execute consequential action.
- TEMPUS offers windows and rhythm without urgency, penalties, missed-opportunity pressure, or causal astrological claims.
- Vitae recognizes stabilization retrospectively. It does not cause, accelerate, sell, or reward becoming.
- Vitae may qualify a participant for bounded responsibility envelopes; it may not gate dignity, belonging, basic participation, or worth.
- MANA may purchase optional support, tools, content, storage, compute, and other utility. It may not purchase recognition, readiness, authority, time acceleration, or human value.
- Geometry and symbolic number may organize an experience. They do not authorize software, prices, permissions, governance, or truth claims by symmetry alone.

### Settled for continued design, awaiting canonical promotion

- The personal orientation grammar uses 12 life domains, 7 rhythmic lenses, and 5 embodied states.
- The five embodied states include an optional sensory layer.
- A journey advances through short frontier actions and reflection rather than a long fixed quest chain.
- A participant may hold one primary active journey in each life domain as the baseline breadth model.
- HOPE, TEMPUS, and Vitae remain distinct faculties around sovereign Identity: reflection, temporal context, and retrospective recognition.

### Candidate, not ratified

- One optional Rite Envelope may be bound to each materialized Vitae section.
- More capable HOPE profiles may appear through capability envelopes and personal authoring tools.
- Additional concurrent journeys inside one domain may consume MANA as breadth utility after economic simulation and governance review.
- The Human Architect's personal deployment may serve as the first end-to-end reference participant for this track.

### Not accepted as written

- Automatic Architect permissions after a raw count of 3,600 completed rites.
- Lower MANA prices merely because a participant holds a higher Vitae grade or appears spiritually advanced.
- Treating spiritual revelation, geometry, or symbolic correspondence as sufficient authority for prices, permissions, governance, security, or Treasury behavior.

## Human promise

The first experience should feel as though conversation has brought the Arcanum to life.

HOPE should:

1. listen for a desire, obligation, pressure, relationship, or opening;
2. show the participant what it believes it heard;
3. ask one natural question that improves understanding;
4. place the confirmed material into a private, revisable life map;
5. offer a small frontier of next actions rather than a fixed destiny;
6. let the participant choose one action and, if useful, shape it as a TEMPUS rite;
7. receive the result or reflection without judging success;
8. update the map so the next action can change with lived reality.

The core loop is:

```text
desire or concern
    -> conversational understanding
    -> confirmed journey
    -> one next action
    -> optional TEMPUS rite
    -> lived action
    -> evidence or reflection
    -> revised orientation
```

## First Node and Architect distinction

### Seed Node Alpha

`Seed Node Alpha` is the existing technical milestone for sovereign infrastructure. It proves local runtime, identity, storage, synchronization, and related substrate behavior.

### First Human Reference Node

`First Human Reference Node` is the working term in this track for the Human Architect's personal, consented use of the complete HOPE–TEMPUS–VITAE loop.

It is a reference participant and deployment, not a universal template. Personal records used for design validation remain private by default and do not become public canon, training data, or a profile imposed on later participants.

This working name must not silently replace `Seed Node Alpha`.

### Personal Architect

Every participant is the Personal Architect of their own Arcanum. This means they may author or revise their own domains, journeys, rites, symbolic lenses, privacy boundaries, and presentation without gaining authority over anyone else.

### Human Architect

The Human Architect stewards cross-layer coherence during Pre-Genesis under the existing doctrine and repository-governance constraints. This is a system role, not a class of superior person.

### Architect-level capability

An Architect horizon becomes desirable when it means greater authorship and stewardship:

- compose personal Rite Envelopes and reusable rite patterns;
- create or adapt private HOPE profiles and reflective lenses;
- connect local tools, calendars, records, and node capabilities with explicit consent;
- inspect provenance, permissions, and audit receipts;
- build private automations with visible limits and revocation;
- draft bounded module or system improvements for review;
- operate a local or personal node;
- contribute to reviewed branches or stewardship processes when separately entrusted.

Architect capability never means access to another participant's private interior, automatic governance supremacy, prophetic authority, or freedom from review.

The 12 life domains are a modern, participant-facing adaptation of the twelve-sign zodiac sequence. The zodiac supplies an optional cultural-symbolic ordering; it does not define personality or destiny.

| 3 | Learning & Exchange | Gemini | What must be learned, named, communicated, or connected? |
| 4 | Home & Belonging | Cancer | What creates safety, family, memory, and a place to belong? |
| 5 | Creation & Joy | Leo | What wants expression, play, courage, visibility, or celebration? |

Domain labels are defaults. A participant may rename or hide them while preserving stable internal identifiers where continuity requires them.

## The seven rhythmic lenses

TEMPUS may offer the classical seven as verbs and questions, not personality types.

| Weekly order | Body | Rhythmic operation | Prompt posture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sun | Center / illuminate | What needs coherence or clear orientation? |
| 2 | Moon | Receive / remember / tend | What is being felt, received, remembered, or cared for? |
| 3 | Mars | Mobilize / protect / separate | What action, defense, boundary, or decisive cut is needed? |
| 4 | Mercury | Clarify / learn / exchange | What needs naming, study, translation, negotiation, or communication? |
| 5 | Jupiter | Enlarge / connect / contextualize | What larger meaning, generosity, judgment, or possibility is present? |
| 6 | Venus | Value / relate / harmonize | What is valued, joined, appreciated, beautified, or brought into accord? |
| 7 | Saturn | Contain / structure / sustain | What limit, duty, standard, endurance, or durable form must hold? |

Two valid sequences must remain explicitly distinct:

- TEMPUS weekly orientation: Sun → Moon → Mars → Mercury → Jupiter → Venus → Saturn.
- Vitae instructional chapter arc: Moon → Mars → Mercury → Jupiter → Venus → Saturn → Sun.

The first follows the ordinary weekday sequence. The second is the existing Moon-to-Sun curricular arc. Neither sequence silently replaces the other.

## The five embodied states and senses

| Order | State | Function | Sense | Experience cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Spirit | Orientation | Smell / atmosphere | Breath, ambience, whole-presence recognition |
| 2 | Air | Discernment | Hearing | Voice, rhythm, language, distinction |
| 3 | Fire | Intention | Sight | Light, image, direction, chosen focus |
| 4 | Earth | Embodiment | Touch | Weight, texture, boundary, concrete action |
| 5 | Water | Integration | Taste | Assimilation, response, completion, what remains |

The embodied cycle is:

```text
Spirit senses
    -> Air names
    -> Fire chooses
    -> Earth acts
    -> Water integrates
    -> Spirit reorients
```

Historical disclosure is required. Fire/sight, Air/hearing, Water/taste, and Earth/touch have strong traditional correspondence. Smell has historically occupied more than one position. Assigning smell/atmosphere to Spirit is an explicit Arcanum design adaptation, not a claim of ancient unanimity.

## The 12 × 7 × 5 orientation grammar

The personal orientation field contains:

```text
12 life domains × 7 rhythmic lenses × 5 embodied states = 420 orientation frames
```

A frame is a question-generating coordinate, not a task quota, fate map, or score.

The sensory layer enriches the five states. It does not multiply the grid into another scoring dimension.

Example:

```text
Home & Belonging
× Saturn / sustain
× Earth / touch

Possible prompt:
What small physical change would make home more stable this week?
```

## Domain Journey contract

Each life domain may hold one primary active Journey.

Each active Journey exposes a Domain Frontier:

```text
home domain
current desire or condition
one frontier action
one optional TEMPUS rite or window
current rhythm
current embodied state
optional sense cue
latest evidence or reflection
next review point
```

A Journey may link to other domains without consuming another active slot. Cross-domain links should not cost MANA merely because life is interconnected.

The complete board may hold up to twelve frontier actions, one per domain. TEMPUS should normally foreground one anchor action and no more than two supporting actions so the board does not become a wall of obligation.

Additional concurrent Journeys inside the same home domain remain an economic design candidate. If MANA is used, it grants optional breadth or infrastructure, never faster becoming, better care, better outcomes, or higher worth.

## Vitae's 3,600-position mathematical lineage

The existing design-candidate Vitae registry defines:

```text
1 class overview + (7 chapters × 5 sections) = 36 indexed positions per class
10 classes × 36 positions = 360 indexed positions per grade
10 grades × 360 positions = 3,600 indexed positions in the intended core curriculum
```

Equivalently:

```text
10 grades × 10 classes × ((7 chapters × 5 sections) + 1 overview) = 3,600
```

This is a real structural derivation. It is a curriculum index capacity, not proof that 3,600 sections are currently materialized and not proof that a participant has completed 3,600 rites.

### Rite Envelope candidate

Each materialized Vitae section may offer one optional Rite Envelope:

```text
RiteEnvelope
- source grade / class / chapter / section
- learning or practice intention
- selected life domain, when personally applied
- TEMPUS context or participant-chosen window
- rhythmic lens
- embodied state
- optional sensory cue
- one bounded action
- evidence or private reflection
- consent and visibility
- completion state
```

If every intended section eventually carries one envelope, the completed core corpus could contain up to 3,600 primary Rite Envelopes. That count remains a content architecture, not a compulsory performance quota.

### Separation of section, rite, recognition, and permission

```text
section viewed or studied
    is not the same as
rite chosen or completed
    is not the same as
practice stabilized
    is not the same as
Vitae recognition
    is not the same as
system permission entrusted
```

Each transition needs its own evidence and authority boundary.

Lowercase `rite` means a voluntary practice or action. Uppercase `RITES` means the separate, optional non-transferable participation-credit primitive. Completing a rite must not automatically mint RITES unless a bounded policy separately authorizes it.

## HOPE capability growth

HOPE may become more capable without becoming more authoritative.

Candidate personal capability envelopes:

| Envelope | Participant experience | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H0 — Presence | Reflect, clarify, and hold one immediate frontier | No hidden long-term profile |
| H1 — Journey Companion | Confirm a Journey and revisit its next action | Memory remains local/private by default |
| H2 — Domain Weaver | Maintain multiple domain frontiers and show cross-domain links | No productivity or worth score |
| H3 — TEMPUS Integrator | Shape actions into optional rites and revisit them through time | No cosmic urgency or causal prediction |
| H4 — Rite Composer | Create reusable personal rite patterns and profile lenses | Personal authorship only by default |
| H5 — Personal Architect Studio | Build private profiles, local automations, integrations, and node workflows | External effects require separate capabilities |

These names are experience-design candidates, not Vitae grades and not public authority states.

System-affecting responsibilities remain governed by the existing Vitae authority-envelope model. A raw section, rite, or grade count may be supporting evidence, but not the sole unlock condition.

A permission decision should have the form:

```text
eligible(permission) =
  prerequisites_met
  AND evidence_sufficient
  AND review_passed
  AND consent_current
  AND safety_clear
```

The participant should always be told what capability is available, why it is available, what it can affect, and how it can be paused or revoked operationally.

## MANA and price boundary

The literal rule “higher Vitae level receives lower prices” is incompatible with Vitae's economic sterility and anti-prestige doctrine. It is therefore not accepted as a current design rule.

A lawful utility price may instead be grounded in actual service cost:

```text
MANA cost =
  measured compute
  + measured storage
  + network or review cost
  + governed service margin
  - transparent subsidy
```

Any production formula requires units, data sources, a floor, a ceiling, a cost basis, an owner, a version, a simulation record, and governance approval.

Potentially compatible ways for sophisticated personal profiles to cost less include:

- local-first execution that measurably uses less shared compute;
- reusable cached context that measurably reduces service consumption;
- prepaid or reserved capacity available on equal terms;
- scholarships or accessibility subsidies;
- contribution credits governed separately from Vitae recognition;
- a free meaningful path that remains useful without advanced paid tooling.

The system must not call a lower price evidence that a person is wiser, more ready, more worthy, or more spiritually advanced.

## Dual provenance standard

Every important claim must name the kind of support it has.

| Provenance lane | Appropriate use | Cannot justify alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematical proof | Counts, topology, transformations, exact derivations | Human meaning, prices, readiness, authority |
| Empirical evidence | Astronomy, usability, safety, resource cost, measured outcomes | Ultimate meaning or metaphysical truth |
| Historical / traditional evidence | Accurate accounts of inherited symbols and practices | Current causation, universal truth, governance |
| Symbolic design synthesis | Names, metaphors, reflective questions, optional rites | Institutional consequence or scientific claims |
| Personal revelation | Participant-authored meaning and private orientation | Rights over others, pricing, governance, security |
| Ratified doctrine / governance | Rights, constraints, authority, permission process | Claims beyond its stated scope |

When mathematical structure and symbolic revelation both inform a design, both lineages should be recorded without allowing either to masquerade as the other.

## Private personal model

HOPE should not secretly become the owner of a permanent psychological dossier.

The Personal Arcanum or local ARCnet node holds consented continuity. HOPE receives only the slice needed for the present interaction.

Suggested fact states:

```text
heard       — HOPE believes the participant said this
confirmed   — the participant accepted or edited it
evidenced   — an external or participant-provided record supports it
superseded  — a later state replaced it without erasing provenance
```

Every remembered item should show its source, privacy, freshness, and edit/delete path. Blank and unknown states are valid.

## New-participant experience

1. **First conversation:** HOPE reflects one desire or concern and shows what it heard.
2. **First confirmation:** the participant corrects or confirms the emerging domain and Journey.
3. **First frontier:** HOPE offers one small next action with alternatives or silence.
4. **First rite:** the participant may shape the action into a personal TEMPUS rite.
5. **First integration:** HOPE receives a Water-state reflection and visibly updates the Journey.
6. **Living board:** other domains appear only as conversation makes them useful; the experience does not begin as a twelve-part questionnaire.
7. **Vitae bridge:** a capability gap can reveal relevant curriculum, practice, or teaching without claiming that the curriculum causes recognition.
8. **Architect horizon:** early preview shows that the participant can eventually compose rites, profiles, local tools, and integrations.
9. **Stewardship boundary:** system-affecting abilities appear only through explicit evidence, review, and permission envelopes.

## Acceptance criteria for a later prototype

- A new participant can begin by speaking naturally rather than completing a questionnaire.
- HOPE displays what it heard and allows correction before durable use.
- The participant receives no more than one anchor and two supporting actions at once.
- Every action can remain ordinary or become an optional rite.
- TEMPUS presents timing as a window, never pressure.
- A reflection changes the next action without rewriting prior history.
- The 12/7/5 coordinate and its provenance are inspectable but not required for ordinary use.
- A Vitae section may offer a Rite Envelope without implying recognition or permission.
- Personal Architect capabilities feel desirable because they increase authorship, privacy, composition, and local control.
- No price, authority, or right is derived from symbolic identity, spiritual claim, grade label, or raw rite count.

## Current conclusion

The strongest coherent model is:

```text
Identity remains sovereign.
HOPE helps the person understand and choose.
TEMPUS helps the person place action in humane rhythm.
Vitae teaches and later recognizes stabilized capability.
The Personal Arcanum remembers with consent.
MANA funds optional utility without purchasing becoming.
Architect capability expands authorship and stewardship without creating superior worth.
```

The 3,600-position Vitae structure is mathematically grounded as curriculum indexing. The next design task is to prove a single honest end-to-end loop before treating 3,600 positions as 3,600 rites or binding any permission, price, or prestige to the count.
