# 5. Exact typed-cell complementarity

This comparison produces a real mathematical bridge.

A regular icosahedron contains:

```text
12 vertices
30 edges
20 faces
```

The Tempus carrier uses the **12 vertices** and a selected subset of **12 edges**.

The Vitae corpus atlas can use the **20 faces**.

This is not a count rescue. Both cardinalities were independently established before the unification test.

The strongest formulation is therefore:

> **Shared Solid, Typed Cells.**

One abstract icosahedral carrier may host Tempus and Vitae only because they occupy different cell types with independently established counts.

# 6. The outer dodecahedron supplies an exact 20 ↔ 20 bridge

Dodecahedron/icosahedron duality is symmetric:

```text
12 dodecahedron faces   ↔ 12 icosahedron vertices
20 dodecahedron vertices ↔ 20 icosahedron faces
30 dodecahedron edges   ↔ 30 icosahedron edges
```

The prior Tempus dodecahedron audit explicitly found **no canonical twenty-part Tempus vertex set**.

That negative result becomes useful here.

The twenty dodecahedral vertices may remain semantically inert as Tempus while acting as geometric **dual pointers** to the twenty inner icosahedron face slots used by the Vitae corpus atlas.

This creates a legitimate rendering transform:

```text
outer Tempus dodeca vertex
        ↕ exact dual pointer
inner Vitae icosa face
```

but it does not create:

```text
Tempus state ↔ Vitae state
zodiac meaning ↔ Grade
zodiac meaning ↔ Specialization
```

Candidate law:

> **A Dual Pointer Is Not a Correspondence.**

# 7. Why vertex-face incidence cannot become meaning

Each icosahedron vertex touches five faces.

Each face has three vertices.

Therefore the complete vertex-face incidence count is:

```text
12 × 5 = 20 × 3 = 60
```

If geometric incidence were treated as semantic influence, every Tempus zodiac anchor would automatically acquire five preferred Vitae regions.

No repository source defines such a 12 × 5 correspondence.

Result: **automatic sign → five Vitae regions — FAIL.**

Candidate law:

> **Incidence Is Not Influence.**

Similarly, the thirty icosahedral edges cannot become Vitae dependencies. The prior Vitae audit already rejected fixed degree-three adjacency as curriculum structure.
