## A. The governing narrative

**One human story at two scales.** A homepage octave contains eight positions and seven transitions. “Discover” allows the visitor to enter five nested narrative octaves, each with eight positions and seven transformations. The five are **HOPE → TEMPUS → VITAE → MANA → ARCnet**. A visitor may explore one, explore all in sequence, or keep scrolling; no forced navigation.

**Pillar invitations:** HOPE — *Share Your Truth*; TEMPUS — *Synchronize With Life*; VITAE — *Discover the Mystery*; MANA — *Empower Your Growth*; ARCnet — *Connect to Something Bigger*.

**Narrative movement:** Awareness → Rhythm → Growth → Empowerment → Connection. Once the pillar story is told, the public homepage changes from explaining possibilities to offering participation: Agency → Follow the Work → Journeys → Take the Arcanum with you.

This is public storytelling. It must not misstate the current state of the network, economic system, curriculum gates, software downloads, or people's rights.

## D. Visual system: invariant design rules [AGREED DIRECTION]

1. **One uninterrupted geometric identity:** a fundamentally **two-dimensional Seed of Life**, widening toward a Flower-of-Life-like larger field where called for; **no hard scene resets** between beats, pillars or major homepage scenes.
2. **Gold = living curved geometry:** circles, curved paths, spirals, luminous points, cyclical movement and the continuity of individual experience.
3. **Seven colors = straight structural connections:** relational lines may pulse, highlight, steady or recede; preserve their seven-color vocabulary. Do not recolor the gold circles as network edges.
4. **Motion carries meaning:** HOPE becomes awareness, TEMPUS becomes rhythm, VITAE grows capability, MANA conveys reciprocal voluntary exchange, ARCnet reveals scale and relational possibility. Reuse the same underlying forms.
5. **The individual remains whole:** distinct circles retain their identity during sharing, exchange and participation. Connections do not require surrender of agency.
6. **Eight positions, seven transformations:** visually and verbally present as an octave; the end can become the next beginning without imposing repetition.
7. **Scroll-driven but never coercive:** a visitor can stop, enter a destination, return, and make choices. Provide readable non-geometric access and reduced-motion behavior; symbolism does not dictate permissions or personal worth.

### D1. The key post-ARCnet transition (primary route)

**ARCnet beat 8:** “Behind every connection, a human story continues.”

Rather than zooming straight back into the HOPE eye, keep scrolling **down**. The expanded planar lattice gently releases: the seven-colored structural lines fade or recede; golden circles disperse into individual lights without being destroyed. One light becomes prominent; other possible paths remain. The composition opens again around voluntary paths.

**Agency:** “Your path, your pace.”

This makes agency the natural conclusion of understanding the five pillars. A return-to-HOPE control remains available separately, for visitors who want to re-enter the cycle. Do not make that the default.

### D2. Agency → Follow the Work

Golden lights begin reconnecting and straight seven-colored lines form again, but **the network is visibly unfinished**. Some relationships are established while others remain unmade possibilities. This should express the truth of current construction rather than market ARCnet as complete.

**Follow the Work:** “See how the network takes shape.” Link to actual development records, updates and evidence with honest status labels.

### D3. Follow the Work → Journeys

The view follows one highlighted connection back toward one golden circle. A familiar golden spiral starts to grow from within that circle. Where HOPE used it to suggest expression and connection, here it suggests carrying learning into life.

**Journeys:** “Carry the light into the world.” The visitor moves from watching a build into the possibility of personal participation. Treat the **Arcanum Journeys page** and **The Great Journey** as related but distinct destinations until routing is explicitly resolved.

### D4. Journeys → Take the Arcanum With You

The gold spiral moves outward; the larger Seed of Life is briefly seen again. The entire gold-and-prismatic system **contracts into a compact Arcanum symbol** without changing its logic. On mobile this can visually become something the visitor might carry in their hand.

**Final:** “Take the Arcanum with you.” A download action must accurately reflect verified availability, not imply an unavailable or unchecked binary.

### D5. Alternate loop: ARCnet → HOPE

On explicit visitor choice, ARCnet's many circles recede and attention approaches one circle. That golden circle becomes the familiar eye; the eye opens as HOPE begins: “Hope is remembering the light within.” The visitor's newly understood network adds meaning to the same image. No force-navigation or auto-repeat.
