# Founder-published Alpha and community transition

Status: Human-approved policy amendment, November 22, 2026; pending canonical integration and Alpha packaging. No entity formation, appointment, election, economic activation or release is effected by this document.

## Present scope

Seed Node Alpha is prepared for founder publication to an invitation-only review group while the nonprofit develops. Founder leadership covers development, releases and bounded collaboration. It does not establish legal membership, an operating institutional board, sovereign-state status, or unilateral shared-treasury authority. Existing Treasury and Economic constitutional restrictions remain controlling.

## Participant protections

Participation is voluntary; belief, donation, endorsement and Vitae completion are not conditions of Alpha review. Participants retain their creations and private records. No compulsory disclosure of reflections or automatic diagnostic sharing. Good-faith criticism and departure are not misconduct. Access restrictions require stated reasons and an opportunity to respond, with narrowly scoped urgent measures and subsequent review. Leaving must preserve authorized personal copies and practical export/recovery; no punitive remote erasure.

Provide a support/complaint contact: acknowledge within five business days and provide a reasoned response within fifteen, or explain delay and a revised date. Define business days and contact details before invitations. Founder review of complaints about the founder is not independent adjudication; offer mutually acceptable independent review where feasible, disclose limitations and preserve external remedies. Higher-risk expansion requires adequate independent escalation capacity.

## Review clock

T0 is the first external reviewer's receipt of the accepted founder-published Alpha. Record its date; no T0 is inferred from development builds. First governance review is due within 90 calendar days of T0, with recurring reviews every 90 calendar days thereafter while founder-led. Review sooner before public distribution, payments/fundraising, shared participant custody, live MANA or representations of legal voting membership. Review alone does not authorize expansion.

Each review records actual authority, privacy-safe complaints/feedback, custody, curriculum and institution readiness, missed milestones and revised actions. Share a privacy-safe report with reviewers. Seek conflict-free scrutiny where available and disclose its absence. A delay does not suspend participant protections or silently expand founder powers.

## Community transition

The commitment is transition under published qualification and admission conditions, not solely founder preference. Complete an approved Architect pathway and establish independent recognition, admission and appeals. No wealth, personal loyalty or private-reflection dossier may substitute for qualification. Later curriculum expansion must not continually move the target for an already approved pathway.

The institutional design retains the first-election trigger: admission of the fifth qualified active Architect voting member starts the 90-day election window, subject to finalized lawful governing procedures and any earlier legal requirements. Earlier admitted members retain rights. This election clock is distinct from the Alpha review clock; no discretionary reset is permitted.

Qualified candidates emerging before legal admission is possible must be recorded and prompt a formation/transition review with specific published steps. Neither indefinitely withholding formation nor unfinished future curriculum may become a silent veto. Actual recognition standards, review appointments and governing instruments must exist before those functions are represented as operational.

The earlier three-director and separate oversight-panel design remains an institutional plan requiring lawful implementation and explicit startup staffing reconciliation; it is not represented as operative during this private founder-led project. Elections transfer institutional authority to elected directors and reserved powers to members. Founder creative/executive service may continue under an agreed mandate without permanent unilateral override. Independent oversight timing and founder-unavailability arrangements must be explicitly resolved before relying on them.

## Ownership and scope boundaries

Founder-existing Vitae, software and artwork remain founder-owned subject to verified rights and valid prior licenses. Institutional governance cannot expand the founder's license by vote. Participant distribution permissions and the future nonprofit license are separate. New substantive contributions require prospective ownership/use terms; no blanket assignment inferred from feedback or participation. Participant information is not automatically transferred to a new operator.

Alpha has no live MANA economy. Full Vitae, commercial services, redemption, hardware and long-horizon community projects remain on the separately scoped roadmap. Final rights clearance, exact-candidate verification, Human acceptance and explicit publication authorization remain release gates.
