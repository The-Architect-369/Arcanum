\# License \& Attribution



> \*\*Proposal — subject to community governance approval.\*\* Until ratified, treat this as the intended licensing posture.



\## 1. Documentation



\- \*\*Documentation (White Pages and docs):\*\* Creative Commons \*\*CC BY 4.0\*\* (attribution required)



\## 2. Code samples



\- \*\*Reference code samples (if included in docs):\*\* MIT License



\## 3. Creator content



\- \*\*Creator content published through Arcanum/ARCnet surfaces:\*\* creator-owned by default  

\- creators may optionally select alternate licenses per work (where the UI supports it)



\## 4. Attribution



Attribution text:



\- “Arcanum Documentation” and/or “Arcanum White Pages”

\- Include a link to the canonical repository or release page



Copyright:



\- © The Arcanum community and contributors


## Permission scope clarification — 2026-11-22

This document records proposed licenses only. It is not a blanket grant for repository code, curriculum, artwork or third-party material. Effective permissions require an applicable rights-holder grant with defined asset scope. Existing valid licenses remain in force. Creator ownership is not transferred by participation in Arcanum.

The preceding licensing and attribution wording is retained as historical proposal text, not evidence that a CC BY or MIT grant was adopted. The collective copyright attribution above does not establish collective ownership or transfer a creator's rights. Reference-code proposals do not license the entire application. Restricted Alpha review permissions apply only to their specified edition and audience; they do not license full Vitae, future editions, or third-party works.

This clarification does not certify exclusive copyright in AI-generated components, ratify the proposed licenses, or revoke an existing valid grant. Applicable third-party notices and licenses remain separately binding.
