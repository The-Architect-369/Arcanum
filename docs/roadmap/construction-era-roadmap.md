# Construction Era Roadmap

## Purpose

This document defines the Construction Era development structure following historical Waves I-XXIV.

CE-W01 and CE-W02 are complete and certified. The current implementation wave is **CE-W03 — Hope at the Center / Arcanum Native Vertical Slice**.

This roadmap is planning authority only. CE-W03 implementation authority is separately recorded in GitHub issue #49 and the synchronized 2026-09-05 decision handoff.

## Era naming and provenance

`Construction Era` is the formal era name.

`Creation Era` is the historical name of the exploratory research phase formerly carried on `docs/creation-era-architecture-trail`. CE-W01 completed the disposition/synthesis audit of that lineage. Unpromoted research remains historical evidence and does not silently become canon.

## Development model

Construction proceeds through three synchronized lanes.

### Lane G — Geometry & Mathematics

Answers: **What is the formal structure of ARCnet?**

Responsibilities include coordinate systems, polyhedral/graph topology, vertices/edges/faces, transforms/projections, formal ARCnet stellar-frame mathematics, permitted cross-system geometric relationships, machine-readable registries, falsification, and geometry-free equivalence.

Geometry may describe or render registered relationships. It does not manufacture semantic authority, capability, identity, recognition, governance power, economic preference, or protocol finality.

### Lane E — Embodiment & Visual Experience

Answers: **How does a human perceive and inhabit the system?**

Responsibilities include Hope-centered experience, Arcanum navigation, visual projection, Vitae/Guardian embodiment, Tempus presentation, motion/orientation/accessibility, and geometry-free human interfaces.

Visual representation is not mathematical or institutional authority.

### Lane A — Architecture & Technology

Answers: **What computation makes the system real?**

Responsibilities include the shared Rust local runtime, Android native host, sovereign identity/storage boundaries, local events/receipts, application registration/capabilities, device/peer synchronization, ARCnet protocol connectivity, signed applications, and later resource/economic/governance infrastructure.

Runtime behavior remains subordinate to doctrine and explicit capability/authority contracts.

## Tempus — cross-lane temporal axis

Tempus is not a fourth peer lane. It supplies temporal coordinate, observer frame, source provenance, cycle context, and synchronization semantics across all three lanes.

- Geometry defines temporal coordinates/transforms.
- Embodiment renders time as context/rhythm without coercive urgency.
- Architecture captures, persists, transports, and only when explicitly required settles temporal witnesses.

Time does not judge. Non-participation carries no penalty. Astronomy/symbolism does not grant permission. Local temporal observation does not require chain finality.

## Registry fabric

Registries are cross-cutting binding/control fabric, not a fourth lane. They may establish stable identifiers, provenance, lifecycle, dependencies, explicit capability/authority boundaries where doctrine permits, machine relationships, and projection metadata.

A registry must not infer human worth, personality, readiness, destiny, authority, or recognition from geometry, timing, completion count, or symbolic correspondence.
