# Construction Era Roadmap

## Purpose

This document defines the Construction Era development structure that follows historical Waves I–XXIV.

Historical Waves I–XXIV remain unchanged. Construction Era numbering restarts locally at Wave 1 and uses stable identifiers such as `CE-W01` to prevent ambiguity with the historical wave sequence.

This roadmap is an implementation-candidate planning authority. It does not by itself authorize runtime, protocol, economic, governance, treasury, or production mutation.

## Era naming

`Construction Era` is the formal era name.

`Creation Era` is retained as the historical name of the exploratory research phase represented by branch `docs/creation-era-architecture-trail`. That research remains evidence for consolidation and does not silently become canon.

## Development model

Construction proceeds through three synchronized development lanes.

### Lane G — Geometry & Mathematics

Answers:

> What is the formal structure of ARCnet?

Responsibilities include:

- coordinate systems;
- polyhedral and graph topology;
- vertices, edges, faces, symmetry, transforms, and projections;
- mathematical definitions for the ARCnet stellar frame;
- formal relationships among Hope, ARCnet, Tempus, Vitae, and permitted geometric overlays;
- machine-readable geometry registries;
- falsification tests and geometry-free equivalence.

Geometry may describe or render registered relationships. It does not manufacture semantic authority, capability, identity, recognition, governance power, economic preference, or protocol finality.

### Lane E — Embodiment & Visual Experience

Answers:

> How does a human perceive and inhabit the system?

Responsibilities include:

- Hope-centered experience;
- Arcanum navigation;
- visual projection of geometric structures;
- Vitae and Guardian embodiment;
- Tempus presentation;
- Nexus and later application relationships;
- motion, transition, orientation, accessibility, and reduced-motion equivalents;
- geometry-free cards, lists, trees, and standard navigation for every required function.

Visual representation is not mathematical authority.

### Lane A — Architecture & Technology

Answers:

> What computation makes the system real?

Responsibilities include:

- shared Rust local runtime;
- Android native host;
- sovereign identity interfaces;
- protected device storage;
- local events and receipts;
- application registration and capabilities;
- trusted device synchronization;
- trusted peer networking;
- ARCnet/Cosmos protocol connectivity;
- signed application packages and manifests;
- later resource contribution, economic, treasury, and governance infrastructure.

Runtime behavior must remain subordinate to doctrine and explicit capability/authority contracts.

## Tempus — cross-lane temporal axis

Tempus is not a fourth peer development lane.

Tempus supplies temporal coordinate, observer frame, source provenance, cycle context, and synchronization semantics across all three lanes.

- Geometry & Mathematics defines temporal coordinate systems and transformations.
- Embodiment renders time as rhythm, context, windows, and observable motion without coercive urgency.
- Architecture captures, persists, transports, and when explicitly required settles temporal witnesses.

Tempus must preserve the canonical Temporal Model:

- time does not judge;
- non-participation carries no penalty;
- astronomical or symbolic context does not grant permission or authority;
- observer, frame, epoch/of-date rule, source/model, and precision remain explicit where astronomical coordinates are used;
- local temporal observation does not require chain finality.

Candidate node flow:

```text
Tempus observation
      ↓
local TempusAnchor
      ↓
signed local receipt
      ↓
optional designated ARCnet witness
      ↓
protocol finality only when required
```

Automatic chain settlement for every clock or ephemeris tick is prohibited.

## Registry fabric

Registries are cross-cutting binding/control fabric rather than a fourth lane.

A registry may establish:

- stable identity of a registered thing;
- provenance;
- lifecycle/status;
- dependencies;
- capability or authority boundaries where the owning doctrine permits them;
- machine-readable relationships;
- projection metadata.

A registry must not infer human worth, personality, readiness, destiny, authority, or recognition merely from geometry, timing, completion count, or symbolic correspondence.

Candidate Construction-era registry family includes:

- canonical module registry;
- Architect agent registry;
- capability registry;
- local application registry;
- trusted device registry;
- trusted peer/relationship registry;
- signed application registry;
- geometry/projection registries;
- Tempus coordinate/provenance registries;
- later resource and economic parameter registries after their authority is ratified.

## Evidence law

Construction uses the development grammar:

```text
idea / intuition
      ↓
exploration
      ↓
bounded arc
      ↓
wave integration
      ↓
milestone achievement
      ↓
evidence
      ↓
Human review / ratification / promotion
      ↓
new stable capability
```

Arcs complete waves. Waves complete milestones.

A wave closes only when evidence appropriate to the claim is present. Numeric symmetry does not determine the number of arcs, waves, applications, modules, or authorities.

## Construction Era wave map

### CE-W01 — Sovereign Coordinate & Runtime Foundation

**Purpose:** Define the center, coordinate systems, temporal anchor contract, and local runtime boundaries from which ARCnet will be constructed.

**Geometry & Mathematics**

- reconcile project geometric terminology with exact mathematical objects;
- formalize the ARCnet stellar frame / stellar-octahedral-cube / Merkabah project model;
- define the sovereign center and coordinate frame;
- define required vertices, edges, faces, symmetries, transforms, overlays, and projections;
- formalize Hope-center relationships and distinguish mathematical structure from symbolic interpretation;
- reconcile Seed-of-Life, octahedral, tetrahedral/cubic, icosahedral, Tree/Vitae, and other retained research at their correct scales;
- establish geometry registry schema and falsification harness;
- require geometry-free equivalent representations.

**Embodiment & Visual Experience**

- establish Hope-centered visual hierarchy;
- define projection from mathematical frame to native visual shell;
- establish visual coordinate and scaling grammar;
- define candidate module/system positioning without creating modules by count;
- define motion, rotation, transition, accessibility, and reduced-motion rules;
- verify visual projection against the mathematical source rather than inspiration alone.

**Architecture & Technology**

- establish Rust local-runtime boundary;
- establish local identity interfaces;
- establish protected-vault/storage contract;
- establish local application registry;
- establish capability isolation;
- establish local event and receipt models;
- establish offline lifecycle and restart verification.

**Tempus axis**

- define `TempusAnchor` contract;
- define clock/source/observer/frame provenance requirements;
- separate local observation from protocol settlement;
- define persistence and receipt semantics that cannot become coercive timing or authorization.

**Exit gate:** CE-W02 may begin only when the native host can be derived from stable coordinate/runtime/temporal contracts rather than recreating them in presentation code.

### CE-W02 — Native Geometric Host

- G: define exact mathematical-to-screen projection and transformation rules.
- E: construct the polygonal/native ARCnet shell faithful to the formal model.
- A: create Android/Kotlin host, narrow Rust bridge, offline lifecycle, and application launcher.

### CE-W04 — Seed Node Alpha

- G: verify geometric implementation against formal definitions.
- E: verify coherent offline embodied experience and recovery.
- A: prove clean install → identity → runtime → Arcanum → Hope → persistence → restart → local receipt.
- Tempus: prove temporal provenance survives persistence/restart and remains local unless explicitly submitted.

### CE-W05 — Personal Constellation

- G: model relationships among user-owned nodes/devices without forced symbolic mapping.
- E: embody owned devices as a coherent personal constellation.
- A: deliberate pairing, selected synchronization, conflict handling, revocation, two-device proof.

### CE-W06 — ARCnet Protocol Connectivity

- G: define topological relationship between local node and wider ARCnet.
- E: distinguish local, submitted, and finalized states visually.

- Tempus: permit explicitly selected temporal witnesses to become protocol-finalized receipts where finality is actually required.

### CE-W07 — Trusted Relational Network

- E: embody trusted relationships without social ranking or human-worth inference.
- A: trusted peer identity, encrypted selected transfer, reconciliation, revocation, abuse/privacy controls.
- Tempus: provide factual ordering/context without inferring intent from timing.

### CE-W08 — Signed Application Constellation

- G: test retained polyhedral/graph overlays against actual application relationships; reject unsupported mappings.
- E: spatially navigate applications without making sacred geometry mandatory chrome.
- A: signed application registry, manifests, provenance, capabilities, install/update/rollback.

### CE-W09 — Application Boundary Canon

- G: stabilize geometry IDs/transforms required across applications.

- A: formal authority/boundary contracts for Commercium, Treasury, Architect, Protection, and other ratified application domains.
- Tempus: define temporal inputs/outputs permitted at each boundary.

### CE-W10 — Multi-App ARCnet Alpha

- G: verify shared geometry remains coherent with a second real application.
- E: prove ARCnet feels like one environment rather than disconnected products.
- A: launch a secondary signed application with isolated state/capabilities and upgrade/recovery proof.
- Tempus: verify cross-app temporal context does not become cross-app authority.

### CE-W11 — Resource Topology

- G: model storage/compute topology and flows mathematically.
- E: make resource contribution understandable without gamifying human worth.
- A: reservation, measurement, proof, reliability, repair, privacy, abuse/energy/device-wear controls, rewardless pilot.

### CE-W12 — Institutional & Economic Structure

- G: model flows and relationships independently from symbolic interpretation.
- E: make Treasury/MANA/governance understandable and transparent.
- A: institutional structure, accounting, MANA policy, Treasury and governance implementation only after explicit ratification/external review.
- Tempus: economic/governance timing remains procedural/factual and cannot become worth or readiness scoring.

### CE-W13 — Community Resource Network

- G: extend topology from personal/peer to bounded community scale.
- E: embody community/resource relationships without flattening participant sovereignty.
- A: limited community storage/compute pilot with contribution receipts and repair.
- Tempus: preserve provenance and service intervals without coercive participation pressure.

### CE-W14 — Productive ARCnet

- G: validate network/flow models under real economic activity.
- E: present economic participation without dominance, ranking, or human-worth mechanics.
- A: productive MANA utility, compensation, Treasury routing, governance-controlled parameters, observability, stress testing.
- Tempus: no time acceleration by payment and no time-based human valuation.

### CE-W15 — Construction Era Closure

- G: full mathematical/geometric coherence audit.
- E: full embodiment, navigability, accessibility, and scale-firewall audit.
- A: sovereignty, runtime, protocol, application, network, resource, economic, and governance audit.
- Tempus: full temporal-integrity audit from local observation through optional protocol finality.

## Ratification posture

The wave titles and dependency spine are planning candidates for Construction Era organization.

Detailed implementation beyond Seed Node Alpha remains progressively evidence-gated. CE-W01 through CE-W04 should reach the highest detail first. Later waves must not silently settle unresolved economics, legal structure, governance thresholds, resource rewards, or arbitrary community executable-code policy.
