# Canonical Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap distinguishes completed capability, current Construction work, approved future direction, and explicitly deferred design. It does not grant implementation authority by itself.

Detailed wave responsibilities live in `construction-era-roadmap.md`.

## Roadmap laws

- One wave has one bounded purpose.
- Planning approval is not execution authorization.
- Future vision is never represented as implemented capability.
- Evidence appropriate to the claim precedes wave closure.
- The Human Architect retains promotion/ratification authority under the applicable doctrine.
- ARCnet preserves separation among protocol, sovereign local runtime, native host/shell, and hosted applications.
- Construction proceeds through Geometry & Mathematics, Embodiment & Visual Experience, and Architecture & Technology.
- Tempus is a cross-lane temporal axis, not a fourth peer lane.
- Registries/provenance are cross-cutting binding fabric.
- Geometry, timing, symbolic correspondence, count, or visual position cannot manufacture authority.

## Historical foundation

Historical Waves I-XXIV established the web/application foundation, repository intelligence, guarded change/evidence tooling, registered read-only agent architecture, production verification, Architect Workbench/Runtime Core experiments, and repository canonicalization work.

Those waves are provenance. Their surviving current rules are encoded in present canonical contracts rather than requiring future work to replay the original wave narrative.
