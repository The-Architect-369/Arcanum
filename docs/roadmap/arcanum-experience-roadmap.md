# Arcanum Experience & Civilizational Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap defines the human-experience, symbolic, curriculum, capability, and civilizational development of Arcanum inside the broader Construction Era roadmap.

It does not grant implementation authority by itself. It distinguishes canonical constraints, implemented state, implementation candidates, planned milestones, visionary hypotheses, and unresolved questions so that future work can proceed without confusing human embodiment, mathematical geometry, and technical infrastructure.

The formal Construction Era roadmap is `construction-era-roadmap.md`.

## Core finding — the embodiment gap

Arcanum does not presently suffer primarily from an absence of content. It has an **embodiment gap**.

The repository already contains constitutional doctrine, canonical modules, deep Vitae curriculum architecture, specialization systems, anatomy and lexicon work, substantial Guardian content, Hope, Tempus, Nexus, Wallet, Architect tooling, and ARCnet foundations.

What remains comparatively underdeveloped is the ability for a participant to **see, navigate, inhabit, connect, practice, and understand those relationships as one coherent world**.

## Relationship to the Construction Era three-lane model

Construction development now uses three synchronized lanes:

1. **Geometry & Mathematics** — formal coordinates, topology, transformations, proofs, and projection registries.
2. **Embodiment & Visual Experience** — the lived and visible human world described primarily by this roadmap.
3. **Architecture & Technology** — local runtime, native host, identity/storage, networking, signed applications, protocol, and later resource/economic infrastructure.

**Tempus** crosses all three lanes as the temporal coordinate/provenance axis. Registries cross all three lanes as binding/control fabric.

This roadmap therefore no longer treats ARCnet Construction and Arcanum Embodiment as the only two post-Wave-XXIV spines. Instead, embodiment is one of three synchronized development lanes, with Geometry & Mathematics made explicit as an independent source of formal structure.

## Status discipline

Roadmap language preserves these distinctions:

- **canonical** — ratified authority or binding doctrine;
- **implemented** — present in a verified repository or deployed surface;
- **implementation-candidate** — approved branch-level or design-level material awaiting promotion or implementation evidence;
- **planned** — approved direction without implementation;
- **visionary** — long-range hypothesis or design horizon;
- **unresolved** — intentionally open and requiring explicit evidence or Human Architect decision.

No symbolic, curricular, economic, governance, geometric, or civilizational proposal becomes canonical merely because it appears in this roadmap.

## Geometry relationship

Geometry is now treated as an independent Construction lane rather than being collapsed into embodiment.

This roadmap may describe how geometry is perceived, navigated, or interpreted, but exact mathematical objects, coordinates, transforms, dualities, projections, and falsification belong to the Geometry & Mathematics authority surface being consolidated for CE-W01.

Governing boundary:

> Visual representation is not geometry itself, and geometry is not authority by itself.

## Symbolic design grammar

The following symbolic grammars remain useful, but they are now subordinate to the broader Geometry & Mathematics audit rather than treated as the complete geometric model of Arcanum/ARCnet.

### Hope — Seed-of-Life / octahedral-centered grammar

**Status: implementation-candidate design grammar requiring CE-W01 geometry reconciliation.**

Hope remains the experiential center of Arcanum. Seed-of-Life relationships may organize presence, reflection, relation, emergence, and generative interior experience where the mathematical and symbolic layers are explicitly distinguished.

Current research also contains an octahedral-centered Hope model. The CE-W01 geometry audit must determine the exact mathematical object, its coordinate relationship to the ARCnet stellar frame, and how any Seed-of-Life spherical representation is derived or symbolically associated.

### Vitae — Tree-of-Life and icosahedral/spiral research

**Status: repository-grounded symbolic/curriculum architecture plus design-candidate geometric projections.**

The Vitae corpus uses Tree-of-Life and planetary organization. The Tree may organize spheres/stations, paths, curriculum, transformation, stabilization, responsibility, and cross-domain knowledge where the curriculum canon supports the mapping.

Do **not** assert that each of the ten Vitae grades maps one-to-one to a Sephirah unless the curriculum canon explicitly establishes that mapping.

Research-branch work also explores an icosahedral corpus atlas and ten-turn/360-position curriculum spiral. These remain separate views answering different questions and must not become progression authority merely because their counts align with curriculum counts.

### Whole ARCnet system — stellar frame / stellar-octahedral-cube / Merkabah project model

**Status: design-candidate formalization target for CE-W01.**

Current research describes an eight-domain ARCnet outer topology, a stable transported stellar frame, inner-octahedral coordination hypotheses, and project terminology around a stellar-octahedral-cube / Merkabah form.

The CE-W01 Geometry & Mathematics lane must reconcile the project vocabulary with exact mathematical definitions and preserve scale firewalls among outer ARCnet system coordinates, inner Arcanum faculties, Vitae curriculum coordinates, Tempus coordinates, and participant Identity.

### Fruit of Life

**Status: visionary / research hypothesis.**

Fruit-of-Life geometry may remain a later comparison/projection candidate, especially where real twelvefold Tempus data or other independently registered relationships justify testing it.

It is not the current whole-system geometry by default and must not be used to create modules, applications, or authorities merely to fill geometric capacity.

## Geometry optionality

Every required participant function must have a geometry-free equivalent using cards, lists, trees, text, standard navigation, and reduced-motion presentation.

Sacred geometry may become directly visible where it helps express meaning, but it is not mandatory chrome across every application surface.

## Epistemic integrity

Arcanum may preserve multiple modes of human inquiry without flattening them into one truth category.

### Empirical science

Examples include anatomy, physiology, biology, chemistry, physics, botany, ecology, astronomy, and material science.

### Historical / traditional knowledge

Examples include historical alchemy, herbal traditions, sacred geometry traditions, planetary symbolism, and occult-anatomy traditions.

Historical existence does not establish every traditional claim as empirically true.

### Symbolic / interpretive practice

Examples include archetypes, correspondences, rites, mythic personification, and contemplative symbolism.

### Subjective / exploratory consciousness

Examples may include meditation, lucid dreaming, altered-state practices, remote-viewing claims, astral-projection claims, and other consciousness exploration.

Arcanum may support responsible inquiry without representing unresolved claims as established empirical fact.

## Participant capability

The key civilizational question is not merely:

> How many users exist?

It is:

> What can this population actually understand, create, maintain, repair, research, teach, or steward?

Potential capability domains include plant and ecological literacy, chemistry and material literacy, fabrication, repair, astronomy, computing, software development, scientific method, food cultivation and preparation, teaching, research, community organization, and infrastructure stewardship.

Vitae can recognize stabilized capability and gate responsibilities affecting others without converting capability into human worth.

## Vitae / MANA boundary

Vitae recognition is **economically sterile**.

MANA may never:

- purchase recognition;
- accelerate readiness;
- bypass a Vitae threshold;
- purchase human worth or status.

Actions around curriculum may independently produce factual receipts under other modules, and separate economy policy may reward eligible participation, contribution, builder work, or infrastructure contribution.

The governing distinction is:

> **Contribution may have economic consequences. Becoming does not have a price.**

Vitae itself does not mint value.

## Civilizational meaning of shared storage and computation

Shared infrastructure should follow actual participant activity.

If participants increasingly perform ecological observation, scientific modeling, plant recognition, astronomical analysis, material simulation, AI inference, research, knowledge indexing, rendering, and application hosting, then shared computation becomes the collective compute requirement generated by a civilization actively learning and building.

Shared storage may hold voluntarily contributed or public scientific datasets, species imagery, ecological observations, educational media, research outputs, public archives, application packages, models, and cultural works.

Infrastructure follows human capability rather than existing as an abstract decentralization feature.
